Timeline

About this concept

What it is

Bolster's Timeline (Jobs → Timeline) as the “Timeline · research” board in Figma draws it (19 Oct), with your answers (22 Oct): every job's plan on a line of days. A job's phases and stages, a stage's labor and materials folded under it, the links between stages, today, and what has no dates yet. The same jobs and dates as the Calendar. Made-up sample data.

Principles

  1. Two parts, one scroll (TL-two-parts). A grid of the work on the left (name, start, working days), its bars on the right, rows lined up. Every fact on a bar is in its row too, so the grid is what a screen reader reads.
  2. Job, phase, stage; items folded (TL-levels). A job's and a phase's dates and progress come from their stages and are drawn as a bracket, never typed, so a stage can't sit outside its job. A stage's labor and materials open under it.
  3. Weeks, Months, Quarters, Fit (TL-scale). Today and the arrows to move; weekends shaded; today a line in the alert colour; lengths in working days.
  4. Moving is dragging (TL-move). Drag a stage to move it, its end to stretch it, a job or phase to move all of it; dates and working days show while you do; the drop saves, with Undo. Moved later, what waits on it keeps its gap (your call, 22 Oct).
  5. Links, finish to start (TL-links). A stage waits for another: an elbow line from one's end to the next's start, drawn by dragging from a bar's end onto another; red and dashed when it starts too early. Named in the stage's summary (Waits for, Holds up), where it's removed. Show turns the lines off.
  6. A click opens a summary (TL-open). A stage's dates, crew, links and Move; a job's span, status and Open job.
  7. Nothing hidden by default (TL-undated). Finished jobs stay, muted, left of today (your call, 22 Oct), Show hides them; jobs with no dates wait under Unscheduled, to schedule from.
  8. On phones, a list (TL-phone). No chart: each job with its phases and stages, their dates, and a track showing where each stage falls in its job. A tap opens the summary as a sheet; no dragging.

Open questions

  • Calendar and Timeline as two pages (assumed, as Bolster has them) or two views of one Schedule page.
  • A stage waits for one other here; Bolster may allow several.
  • Moving an item on its own (items follow their stage here).
  • Days off from the company's settings; weekends here.
