Summary cards

A row of cards above a list (D67): either KPIs, the default, a few figures that say how the list is doing and what needs someone, or the lifecycle, every status a card, endings counted for the last 30 days, for pages where people move rows through their statuses. A card is a button that applies one filter, usually a status (Pending), or a flag or category slice people work on (Missing documents); it stays pressed in while it does, one at a time. Its figure is money where the list's key figure is, the count beside it. The insight card is information, not a filter. Status cards wear the status's mark (see Badge), so the shape says the status and colour only backs it up.

Rules

  • One status card per status a person filters by, in the list's lifecycle order, after All. Cards that aren't statuses become saved views (L-summary).
  • At most one insight card, and only with a figure people act on. Its explanation uses the words on screen.
  • Phones: the insight card leads, so the overview is in the first screen; the row scrolls sideways.
