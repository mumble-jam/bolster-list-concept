Phones: rows with four corners

Below 768 a row becomes an item (D32): the number and a date on a quiet overline, the amount top right, the name across the full width at 16px (it wraps to two lines rather than being cut), then where and the status. No checkbox column: Select, or a long press, starts selecting. This frame is the live list page.

Kinds (D50, D53)

What a row holds, as a column of small icons: a vendor order's labor (hard hat) and materials (box). A concept that recurs across Bolster and matters, so it has a column of its own; on phones the icons lead the row's overline. A row's icons share one tooltip that names them together, “Labor and materials” (D52), in the icons' order; the record says the same in words.
