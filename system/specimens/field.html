Text inputs, text areas, native selects and money inputs share one field frame: label above, control, helper below. Borders clear 3:1 against the page so the control is findable (the as-is border was 2.9:1). Focus turns the border blue with a 1px ring inside it. Errors colour both the border and the helper.

Formats: phone, email, postal code

Typed any way, tidied when the field is left (never while it's typed), by the country it's in: the company's, or an address's own. Wrong, it stays as typed and says why. Try “5550142290”, “+44 20 7946 0958”, “Name@Gmail.COM”, “787041234” or “k1a0b1”, then leave the field.

Numbers and units

Typed, never stepped with buttons: ↑ and ↓ step by the field's step (Shift: ten). The unit sits after the number, in its words. When left, a number is grouped and rounded to its unit's places; money always shows cents. Empty stays empty. Grouping follows the country: 1,250.5 here, 1.250,5 in Germany.

Rules

  • Every control has a visible label. Placeholder text is an example, never the label.
  • Empty values stay empty: never render "null" or "$0.00" for an unset price (the as-is settings page shows "null").
  • Error text says how to fix it, in sentence case.
  • One search field design app-wide (the audit found four).
  • A value is tidied when the field is left, never while it's typed, and only when it's right; wrong, it stays as typed and says why.
  • Phones, postal codes and number grouping follow the country (the company's, or an address's own). A number starting with + is kept international.
  • Numbers are typed; no − and + buttons. The unit goes after the number, in words people say (“sq ft”, “hrs”), never typed into it.
