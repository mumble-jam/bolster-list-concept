Button

One button component, named by role (D4). Primary is Bolster's black button with highlighter text; brand is the yellow call to action for the one step that moves a job forward ("Preview and send"). Blue is not a button colour: it's for links, focus and selection. Every state has a visible focus ring (D8). Primary, brand, danger and secondary have a body: an edge, a light line along the top, a shadow; hover lifts 1px and a press sinks at once, then eases back. Ghost is for places that already say "button" (toolbars, rows, bars, Close). Every button has 8px corners (D33).

Roles and states

Hover, active and focus are pinned with data-state for this page only. Use one primary per view; secondary for everything else, ghost inside toolbars and rows. Tertiary is gone: secondary covers it, lifted on the page and flat on a card (D78).

On the dark top bar

Secondary: lifted on the page, flat on a card (D78)

The surface decides, never the page that uses it: on the page a secondary button has its body (an edge, a darker bottom edge, a white top line, a small shadow) and lifts when pointed at; on anything already raised (a card, a dialog, a popover, a menu, a bar) it lies flat, with the card's edge. Primary, brand and danger keep their body everywhere: there it is the emphasis. Secondary's label is regular, lifted or flat, a step quieter than their medium.

A picker's button reads back what's picked

A button that opens a picker (a popover, a sheet on phones) says what's picked, in a .button-value, its icon quieter and drawn by its height. aria-haspopup names what it opens; while it's open (aria-expanded="true") its edge is the accent. It replaces the field button. A picker that names no icon of its own ends in a chevron, so it reads as one that opens a list.

Icons side by side (D41)

A button's size sets its icon's box, but glyphs fill their boxes differently: × draws 62 % of it, the expand arrows all of it. Icons that sit together match their ink and line instead. One drawn smaller goes a weight up so its line stays as thick, and keeps its own size through --icon-size (ic(name, size, { optical: true }), or data-optical in a specimen). scripts/check-icons.js measures a page.

Rules

  • Labels start with a verb, sentence case, no trailing punctuation: "Create job", not "New" or "CREATE".
  • Icon-only buttons need an aria-label, and a tooltip if the icon isn't universal.
  • Icons side by side match in ink and line, not in box size (D41); scripts/check-icons.js lists the ones that don't.
  • Don't override a button's height, radius or colours locally. If a variant is missing, add it here (the audit found 257 of 364 buttons with local overrides).
  • Show an action only when it applies. A disabled button needs a reason nearby.
  • A row or card that opens something is a link, not a button: see Data table and Card.
  • Secondary is lifted on the page and flat on anything raised: the surface decides (D78). Never pick a flat or a raised one by hand.
  • A button that opens a picker shows the value in a .button-value; its own name (Add filter) stays a command.
  • Red is for what can't be undone. Where Delete is one action of several it's a quiet danger (red text, fills red when pointed at); the filled red danger is only the confirmation's final button.
