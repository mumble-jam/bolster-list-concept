Calendar

A month as a grid you can click or move through with the keyboard, and the date range picker built on it: the list's date filter (D37). It came out of the date range picker research: presets first, a calendar for the rest, and typing always possible. Drawn for 8 Oct 2026, the concept's today, with a span picked from 8 Sep to 3 Oct.

The date range picker

Ten presets in a rail, short spans to long, All time first; they apply at once and close. Custom is everything else: From and To take typing, two months take clicks, and the span waits for Apply. A span that matches a preset is that preset. Days after today are off for a date that can only be in the past, and the foot says which date it filters by.

On phones

The same bottom sheet as every other menu. The presets are the list (All time, Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month), then Custom…, which drills in to From and To, the last twelve months and this one in a column that scrolls, and Apply pinned to the bottom. Try it on the List page's phone frame.

Rules

  • Presets apply at once; a span built by hand waits for Apply.
  • Typing is always possible; a date that isn't one, or is after today for a past date, is flagged and Apply waits.
  • Weeks start on Sunday (US English); days are the company's days, and the foot says so.
  • A saved view or a page description can hold a span relative to today: “..-91” is more than 90 days ago.
