Badge

Small, static labels. Job status has its own token set (D12), so every status reads the same everywhere; "Booked" moves off Tailwind's lime onto Bolster green, and in progress is blue. Status badges carry no dot: the word is the cue, colour the second; a dot isn't an icon, and status icons (the job list's marks) show only where a list asks for them (D61).

Status marks

On list pages a status wears a shape as well as a colour (D28), so the shape says the status and colour only backs it up. Jobs fill a quarter at a time as they move on; a slash is the client saying no. Once the shape carries meaning it's a graphic that must reach 3:1, so draft, pending and in progress have their own darker mark colours.

Rules

  • One or two words, sentence case, no punctuation.
  • Status comes from data-status only; don't pick a tone that "looks right".
  • Badges aren't buttons. A filter chip that toggles is a segmented control or a filter card.
