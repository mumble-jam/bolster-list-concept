Lists are real tables. Each row's name is a link stretched across the row: one click, Enter or a screen reader's link list opens it (as-is rows only opened on double click and weren't reachable by keyboard). Selecting is a checkbox; the bulk bar appears when anything is selected.

Thumbnails

A photo of a thing, such as a catalog item (D55). What sits before a row's name is a slot: an avatar for a person or company (round), a thumbnail for a thing (square), an icon, or nothing, page by page. The photo lies over a placeholder, the image icon on a quiet tint, so while it loads, where there's none, or when its link breaks, the placeholder shows. A list with photos keeps them on phones, down the left of each item; a list without any drops the slot there, as it drops avatars.
