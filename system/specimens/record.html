Rules

  • A row click peeks (this record, beside the list), unless the thing has its own full-screen window (the job workspace, a vendor's window): then the row opens that, and this record is Quick look, second in the row's ··· menu (D69, L-row-click).
  • The activity runs oldest first: a solid line through what happened, a dashed one to the next step (a hollow ring). Job events wear the status mark they moved the job to.
  • Two actions and More on one line; when both don't fit, the second moves into More.
  • Related: a link to every record the thing connects to (the job's client; an order's vendor, job and the job's client), its kind on the left; they stay out of the fields, and View job isn't repeated in More (D57, L-related). The other way round too: what points at the thing, as a count that links to the list (a vendor's 4 vendor orders, 13 expenses), or None yet in plain text.
  • A row with a picture shows it large at the top, with its credit (D55). The record scrolls down, never sideways: a long field value ends in an ellipsis.
  • The header: the record's name, wrapping rather than cut short, then open and close. Open is the expand icon with a tooltip naming the record (“Open job”): the record grows into its full view in the same tab (D40). ↗ only marks links that leave the app.
