Floating bar

Bulk actions for the rows you've ticked (D34). On desktop a dark capsule floats at the bottom of the list, so the rows stay where they are while you select. The count is a chip that holds its own ×, and Escape does the same, so the bar's end is free. Then Select all, the actions with the product's icons beside their labels, and Delete in red after a rule. On phones the bar takes the tab bar's place.

Several jobs ticked

One job, every action

One pending job: its own actions, then More for the rest (Delete lives there for a pending job). From 1100 wide everything fits.

Everything ticked, and below 1100 wide

With all of them ticked the chip says so and the link turns into Unselect all. Below 1100 wide one action stays on the bar and the rest fold into More; the labels stay.

Phones: the tab bar turns into the action bar

Below 768 the page header shows the count with Select all and Done, and the bar sits where the tab bar was, in the chrome's own dark: up to two actions, More, and Delete last, each an icon over its label. Until something is ticked the actions wait, disabled. Try it in the live list on the List page: tap Select, or hold a row.

Rules

  • Only where the list has bulk actions (L-selection); never invent them.
  • Red is for what can't be undone: Delete, last and after a rule. Archive and Delete confirm first; the confirmation's final button is the only filled red.
  • The chip's × and Escape clear the selection; on phones Done does.
  • Actions that depend on what's selected (one job vs several) change with the selection; menus (Change status, More) open from the bar.
