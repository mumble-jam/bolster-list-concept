Search field and chip

What a toolbar finds and narrows with, beside its buttons. The search field is bare until hovered or focused; a chip is one filter, set or not. Add filter and the date range beside them are secondary buttons, flat on the card, the date range reading back its value (Button, D78: they replaced the field button). Together in the table card's header (D21), the to-do's (D72) and the calendar's (D75).

Search field

Bare in a toolbar: no edge, a hover fill, the focus ring on the whole field. It takes the room left in the row.

Chip

One filter of a list. Unset: dashed, with a plus, it opens the filter's picker. Set: solid, the field and its value, and a separate remove button. Hover turns the field and value link blue together; the × goes darker. While its picker is open, its edge is the accent.
