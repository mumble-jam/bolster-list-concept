Icon vocabulary

Icons are meaning, so they belong to the system, not to a page (D58, rule L-icons). One meaning, one icon, everywhere. A field takes its icon from what its key means, else from the record it links to, else from its type; an action from its label, else from its verb. The build writes them into a page's description and the checker holds every page to this list; a meaning it doesn't cover stops the build until an icon is proposed and approved. Seeded from the job list. Proposed marks picks waiting for review. Read from system/model/icons.json, drawn in the menus' size and weight.
