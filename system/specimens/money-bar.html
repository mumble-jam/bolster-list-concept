Money bar

A list's KPIs when its key figure is money split by where the money is (D67, L-summary): on invoices, what's owed (overdue first, then not due yet) beside what came in (paid in the last 30 days, its window named). The figures are the buttons: each filters the list like a summary card, one at a time, its chip named after it. The bar is their picture, hidden from screen readers. What nobody owes yet (drafts) is a quiet line under the card. Designed in Figma (research page, “What earns a summary card”, section 06).

Rules

  • Groups answer separate questions with their own time scopes (owed: everything open; came in: the last 30 days), so they never share one scale.
  • Overdue comes first and in the danger colour; the rest wear their status's colour.
  • A segment is never thinner than 4 px, so a small amount still shows; an empty group keeps its track, as a hairline.
  • The figures follow the list's other filters, like summary cards; the Paid window doesn't follow the date range, and says so.
  • Phones: the groups stack, the figures stay side by side and their lines wrap.
