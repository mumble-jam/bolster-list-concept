States

Every list designs four states besides the normal one: loading, empty (no records yet), no results (filters exclude everything) and error. They sit inside the table card, so the page keeps its shape.

Empty: no records yet

No jobs yet

Jobs you create, or estimates people accept, show up here.

No results

Says what's excluding everything, in the screen's words, and offers to undo it.

No jobs match

Nothing matches Status: Declined, search “skim”. Remove a filter or try a different search.

Error

Jobs didn't load. Check your connection and try again.

Loading

Skeleton rows in the columns' own widths, so nothing moves when the data arrives. They shimmer unless reduced motion is on.
