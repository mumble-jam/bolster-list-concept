States

Every list designs four states besides the normal one: loading, empty (no records yet), no results (filters exclude everything) and error. They sit where the content will be, so the page keeps its shape; loading also covers the summary cards, the record and a page's first load.

Loading

Skeletons stand where the content will be, in its shape and size, so nothing moves when it arrives (D142). They shimmer unless reduced motion is on. The region loading is aria-busy; what's already known (a page's title and toolbar, a record's name and status) shows at once.
