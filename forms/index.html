About this concept

What it is

The forms research's three directions (docs/forms-research.md, “Forms · research” in Figma), each built for real on two of Bolster's forms, so they can be compared by using them: a new job (Bolster's Assign job to customer, then the job's Settings: address, roles, details, payments) and a progress invoice for one job. Your ask, 29 Oct. Everything works; nothing talks to Bolster.

Picked: C (your call, 29 Oct, D140), with A's rules for every field that remains. C now follows its draft rules (docs/forms-c-research.md, 06), for review here before any of them goes into the system: the record read with labels beside values, its head and next step, its source beside it linked both ways, what came from the source said once and only the exceptions marked, empty values saying what they're needed for, values that belong together edited as one, nothing red on arrival, a summary before each step with every suggestion settled on its own, a reason for a strong warning, the invoice kept as the document with the team's note on it, and nothing read lost on leaving.

The three

  1. A · A form done well. A page of fields, everything asked at once, one Save (D134, the system today).
  2. B · The record is the form. A create asks only what the thing can't exist without; then the record is a page you read, each value changing where it is; its state decides how a change saves.
  3. C · Forms that fill themselves. B, starting from what you already have (a voicemail, an enquiry, the job's progress), every value marked with where it came from.

Built from the system

The app's page (D146): the shell's menu and bars, its page header (a screen's title and badge move up into it), its content, the canvas grid behind; which form and which direction are Concept controls, with what each direction means. form.js (checking, the summary, the leave dialog), field.js (formats), combobox.js, address-field.js, the dialog and the badges. New here, and drafts if a direction is picked: a value that changes in place, the marks for where a value came from, the invoice as a document with its states.

Open questions

  • In C, which sources first: calls, website enquiries, photos, supplier quotes?
  • Is a sent invoice changed and resent (as here), or frozen with “Reopen for changes”?
