About this concept

What it is

Bolster's Tasks page as a to-do list, not a list (D71, D72): work to tick off, grouped by when, its fields set where they are. Built after the table builder couldn't carry it (the Tasks list, 19 Oct) and the “To-do lists” research board in Figma.

Principles

  1. The check is the row (T-check). Round, first, one tap: the task is done, struck through where it is, and a toast says so with Undo. Nothing asks first. Selecting is a mode (Select in the header): the checks turn into square boxes, and a bar offers Complete, Due date and Assign.
  2. Done stays in place (T-done). A ticked task keeps its place until the list is next drawn (a filter, a search, the other grouping), then joins Completed, the last group, folded with its count.
  3. Grouped by when (T-when). Overdue in red, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later, No due date; an empty group doesn't show. Job is the other grouping. Each header counts its open tasks: no cards above the list (T-summary). Each group is a card of its own, under the toolbar's, so a day reads as one piece.
  4. Fields change in place (T-in-place). The due date and the assignee are chips that open a picker and save on pick. An empty one shows on hover (a dashed chip, a ghost face), never “Set date” on every row; on phones it's in the task. Priority is a mark before the title, as in Linear: one, two or three bars for Low, Med and High, faint dots for none.
  5. Add where you are (T-add). Add task at the end of each group, filled with its value (due today, this job); Create task in the header for the rest.
  6. Your own order. Drag a task by its handle to reorder it (or Alt and the arrow keys). It starts by priority, then due date. Dropped into another day's group, it takes that day, with Undo; Overdue and Completed take no drops. On phones a long press lifts the task, and it follows the finger.
  7. A task opens beside the list (T-open). From 1280 wide a panel beside it, below that a sheet: its fields, set the same way, and its story.
  8. The table card's toolbar, Bolster's filters. Add filter offers what Bolster's Filter & sort panel does: Show (general tasks, labor tasks, material items; it opens on the first two, as Bolster does), Priority, Assignee, several at once, and a Period of due dates. Each is a chip on the row below with the count and Clear all. Display settings hold Group by (Date, Job). Completed is always the last group, folded, so there's no Show completed tasks.
  9. Trying out: the toolbar on the page (on by default). The bar sits on the page itself above the group cards, so the only cards are the days, in one row: Add filter as an icon and the count after a rule on the left, the search (an icon that opens into a small field, closing again when left empty; Escape empties it) and display settings after a rule on the right. Chips take the row below only when there are some. Switch it off in Concept controls to compare with the toolbar on its own card, the search a bare field across it. Not a rule yet.
  10. The job's schedule fills it. Most tasks are a job's labor, with their hours; some are written by people, some are material items.

Open questions

  • Recurring tasks, subtasks and comments: none were seen on Bolster's page.
