Patterns

Picking dates

Presets first, then a span on two months, and typing always possible. Weeks start on Monday everywhere. The button reads back which date it filters by and the span.

Stablecomponents/calendar.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-11-05

Most date questions have a usual answer (the last 30 days, this month, year to date), so the picker leads with those, applied in one click. Everything else is a span built on two months, or typed. The same calendar and the same week serve every page that picks or shows dates: the list's date filter, the to-do's due dates, the calendar.

The date range picker

Ten presets in a rail, short spans to long, All time first, then Custom. From and To take typing, two months take clicks, and a foot says which date the filter reads, the span's length and the time zone, with Cancel and Apply. Drawn for 8 Oct 2026, with a span picked from 8 Sep to 3 Oct.

How it behaves

StepWhat happens
OpeningThe date button opens the picker under it, focus on the pressed preset.
PresetsA click or Enter applies the preset and closes. ↑ ↓ Home End move between them.
DaysThe first pick starts a span, the second ends it; an earlier second day swaps them. While half built, the band follows the pointer or the focus.
TypingFrom and To read Sep 8, 2026 · 8 Sep 2026 · Sep 8 · 9/8/2026 · 2026-09-08, on Enter or on leaving the field, and the months move to show it. A date that isn't one, or comes after today where only the past makes sense, is flagged in the foot, and Apply waits.
CommittingA span built by hand waits for Apply. Cancel, Escape or a click outside leave the filter as it was, focus back on the button.
Reading backThe button names the date and the span: “Created · Sep 8 – Oct 3, 2026”. A span that matches a preset reads as the preset. The month is a word, so 9/10 can't be read two ways.

Calendar keys

The months follow the WAI-ARIA date picker pattern: the grid is one tab stop, on the focus day, and the keys move the focus day. The months follow it.

KeyMoves to
← →The day before or after
↑ ↓The same day a week before or after
Home, EndThe first or last day of the week (Monday, Sunday)
Page Up, Page DownThe same day a month before or after; with Shift, a year
Enter, SpacePicks the day

Weeks start on Monday

A week starts on Monday, the working week first and the weekend together at its end, everywhere (D76). It's one setting, the shell's (BolsterShell.week: the day names in order, which day of the week a date is, a week's first day), read by everything that draws a month, groups by week or moves by week: the picker's grid and its Home and End, the to-do's This week and Next week, the calendar's views. In the product it would be the company's setting.

At each width

WidthThe buttonThe picker
768 and upThe date it filters by and the span.A popover under the button: the presets' rail, From and To, two months, the foot.
Below 768Only the span; it gives way before the search field.A bottom sheet of six presets, then Custom…, which drills in to From and To, thirteen months that scroll, and Apply pinned to the bottom.

Usage

  • Offer the picker on a list's main date: any list with a date column has a date range (D46).
  • Say which date it filters by, in the foot and on the button once set. Which date that should be (created, or last activity) is still open.
  • Turn off days that can't be picked (after today, for a date that can only be past) rather than flagging them after.
  • Keep “More than 90 days ago” and its kind out of the presets: To covers them.

Accessibility

  • The picker is a role="dialog" named for what it filters (“Filter by created”). The presets are a group of toggle buttons (aria-pressed).
  • Each month is a role="grid" labelled by its title; weekdays are column headers with the full name in abbr.
  • Each day is a button named in full: “Thursday, October 8, 2026, today”. A span's ends carry aria-selected; days that can't be picked are disabled.
  • Today is an outline, a span's ends the inverse fill, the band between them the selected tint, joined across a week and rounded where it starts or stops.

Code

ValueMeans
A preset idOne of the ten presets, All time among them.
from..toA span whose ends are each a date, a number of days from today, or empty. ..-91 is more than 90 days ago.

Saved views and page descriptions store these values. The markup of the calendar and the picker is on the Calendar page.
