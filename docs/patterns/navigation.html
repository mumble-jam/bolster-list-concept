Navigation at each width

One dark menu with every section: folded to an icon rail on desktops, its pages popping out beside it; below 1024, a floating menu button that opens the same menu and pushes the page aside.

Trying outconcepts/shared/shell.jsconcepts/shared/shell.csscomponents/shell.cssUpdated 2026-11-02

Trying out. This navigation is built in the concepts' app shell (concepts/shared/shell.*) and being tested. The system's shell component still draws the first design: an icon rail, a section sidebar and a bottom tab bar (D15). It follows if this stays.

Bolster has one level of navigation for most of its pages: one dark menu with every section, the sections that have pages opening as accordions, and Settings as a window of its own. What changes with the width is how the menu is reached, never what's in it.

At each width

WidthNavigation
1024 and upThe top bar (the company, search, your account) over the menu. The menu is folded to its icon rail by default; opened, it stays open, and that's remembered in the browser.
768 to 1023No rail and no top bar. A floating menu button, bottom left, opens the menu at the pinned menu's width (256) and pushes the page aside by as much. Search, Ask Bolton and your account are in the menu, Help in its foot. The pages keep their desktop layouts.
Below 768No tab bar and no top bar. The same floating button opens the same menu, pushing the page aside to an 88px column. Its rows are 44 tall, its type a step up. Create floats bottom right, across from it (D29).

The menu

  • Every section, each with its icon. Sections with pages (Jobs, Financial, Customers) are accordions: one open at a time, the current page's open when you arrive, a chevron at the row's end turning down as it opens.
  • The pill marks the page you're on, or its section's row while that section is folded shut.
  • The foot (Messages, Files, Help, Settings, then folding) stays put while the rest scrolls under it.
  • Dark in both themes, as the top bar is: it's the app's chrome, not the page.

Folded to the rail

Folded, the menu is a rail of icons that stays its own width (D107):

  • Pointing at a section with pages opens them in a popup beside the rail, 60ms after the pointer arrives, coming in over 70ms: the section's name, its pages with their icons, the page you're on in the pill. Moving to the next section moves the popup there at once.
  • A row without pages (Dashboard, Messages, Settings, Expand menu) shows its name alone in the same popup, a label the pointer passes through.
  • Every popup's name is level with its row's icon. Pointing away from both (80ms), Escape or a choice closes it.
  • Expand menu at the rail's foot pins the menu open; Collapse menu at the menu's foot, under Settings, folds it again.

Below 1024

  • The menu slides in and pushes the page aside rather than covering it. The button rides with the page into the column it leaves, as ✕ (“Close menu”).
  • A tap anywhere in that column, a link, or Escape closes the menu, and the page slides back (320ms).
  • While rows are ticked, the menu button steps aside for the bulk bar: one thing at the bottom at a time.
  • With no top bar, what sticks to the page sticks to the very top (see Page scrolling).

Settings

Settings opens from the menu, the rail or the company menu as a full-screen window with its own navigation: groups, then pages, on the left, the page itself on the right. Close or Escape closes it, and focus goes back where it was.

The top bar

From 1024 wide the top bar holds the company, the search field and your account. It hides as you scroll down into a page and comes back as you scroll up, or when focus goes into it (D73). The search is a real field: ⌘K (Ctrl+K off a Mac) or / from anywhere focuses it (see Global search).

Keyboard

WhereKeys
The railA section's row taking focus opens its popup. Tab or ArrowRight goes in, the arrows move, Escape or ArrowLeft come back to the row.
The menuTab moves through sections and pages; Enter or Space opens and shuts an accordion.
Below 1024The menu button opens and closes the menu; Escape closes it.
Anywhere⌘K, Ctrl+K or / focuses the search.
  • The menu is a <nav>; the page you're on is aria-current="page". Accordions are <details> sharing a name, so opening one closes the other.
  • On the rail, every row shows its name in its popup when pointed at; a section's row opens its popup on keyboard focus too.
  • A shut menu is hidden, so nothing in it takes focus.

Motion

An accordion grows to its height and its pages fade and settle one after another, only when you open it. Hover and the pill ease quickly; a press darkens at once. On phones the ☰ turns into ✕ as the page slides. With reduced motion, none of it moves.

What it replaces

  • Two levels of navigation, an icon rail and a section sidebar beside it (D15), which kept the sidebar open at 768 and squeezed forms to 488px with three-line labels.
  • A bottom tab bar on phones that doubled the menu.
  • A rail that expanded over the page on hover, and a menu that unfolded over the page on hover (D98), which was a surprise when pointing at a section only meant to show its pages.

What's still open

  • D88's title says the top bar is a footer on phones; its body and the shell have no top bar below 1024, only the floating menu button. D98 and I9 give the tablet menu 256 wide; the shell draws 272.
