Global search

One field in the top bar finds any record (customers, jobs, invoices, files, tasks), grouped by type, narrowed by a chip. It finds; it doesn't create.

Trying outcomponents/shell.cssconcepts/shared/search.jsUpdated 2026-11-02

Trying out. The field is in the system's shell (shell.css); the panel it opens is built in the concepts (concepts/shared/search.js) and is a draft waiting for the product designer's approval.

Search in the top bar finds any record by its name, its address or its number, from any page. It's for getting somewhere: type “garage” and open Alex Ferreira's garage drywall job. A list's own search narrows that list; this one crosses them all.

The field

  • A real field you can type in, a well lit in the dark bar, a step brighter under the pointer and again when focused, with a 1px ring in the focus colour and a soft halo.
  • Its shortcut at its end: ⌘ K (Ctrl K off a Mac) as two keycaps at rest, esc while it's focused, Clear (×) once it holds words. Clear empties it and keeps focus.
  • The logo's yellow is its caret and its selection.
  • The field says “Search”, as every search does (D130); its accessible name, “Search Bolster”, says what it reaches.

What opens

A panel under the field, as wide as it (440 at least), on the page theme's raised surface:

WhenThe panel shows
Before typingThe type chips (All, Customers, Jobs, Invoices, Files, Tasks) and the records you opened last.
TypingResults grouped by type, always in one order (customers, jobs, invoices, files, tasks), three of each with See all n, which opens the type's list searched for the words. The typed letters are marked in the highlighter yellow. Then the words themselves as a row. A type with nothing found is dimmed on its chip.
NarrowedOne type, eight rows, its chip shown in the field as a token (“Jobs ×”). Nothing found while narrowed offers the count everywhere.

Each group is an outline card; each row is the type's icon on a small tile, the name and the line under it, then the record's state as its list shows it (a job's status badge, an invoice's amount). The keys are listed at the panel's foot.

What it matches

  • Every word must match, in the name, the line under it, or the digits of an invoice or phone number (three digits or more).
  • A match at the start of a word in the name ranks first.
  • No typo tolerance yet.

It finds, it doesn't create

The panel has no Create job, Create invoice or Add customer, before typing or found by their words while typing (D123). Making something stays where it's made: each page's own Create button. Actions with shortcuts would be a command palette of their own, if ever.

Keyboard

KeyDoes
⌘K, Ctrl+KFrom anywhere: focuses the field, its words chosen.
/The same, where the page has no search of its own.
↑ ↓Move through the options; as you type, the first result is in focus.
EnterOpens the option in focus.
BackspaceAt the field's start, takes off the type token and widens the search again.
EscapeEmpties the field; on an empty field, widens a narrowed search; then closes the panel and leaves.
Tab, a click outsideClose the panel.

At each width

WidthWhere search is
1024 and upThe top bar's field, the panel under it.
Below 1024There's no top bar: search is at the top of the menu, opened by the floating menu button (see Navigation at each width).

Accessibility

  • The field is a combobox: focus stays in it while the arrows move through the options, the one in focus named by aria-activedescendant.
  • The results are a listbox the field controls. The type chips are a group of toggle buttons (aria-pressed); the token in the field has its own ×, named “Search everything”.
  • The keycaps are hidden from screen readers; the shortcut is the field's, not a label's.
  • Clear is a button named “Clear search”, with a tooltip.

Still open

Recent searches as words or as records (records for now), what else should match, a results page for the words' row, and where a record opens: in the concepts, a record opens its list searched for it, since there are no record pages yet.

What it replaces

  • A button dressed as a field that did nothing.
  • A full-screen blurred layer with no dialog role, which dropped focus on the page's body when it closed.
  • Live Bolster's search dialog, whose eight type chips were all on: narrowing meant turning seven off.

<label class="top-bar-search">
  <svg …magnifier…></svg>
  <input type="search" placeholder="Search" aria-label="Search" autocomplete="off" spellcheck="false">
  <button class="top-bar-search-clear" type="button" aria-label="Clear search" data-tip="Clear">…</button>
  <span class="top-bar-search-keys" aria-hidden="true"><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd><kbd data-focused>esc</kbd></span>
</label>

What's still open

  • Recents as words or records, what else should match, typo tolerance and a results page are undecided; a found record opens its list, not the record.
