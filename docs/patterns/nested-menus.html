Nested menus

A menu row with items of its own reads its value and opens a second menu: beside the first on a desktop, drilled into on a phone's sheet. Written once, in the shell, and used by every page that has one.

Stablecomponents/popover.csscomponents/menu.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-10-26

Some menu rows hold a choice of their own: a setting in Display settings (Group by, Row height, Columns), Change status in a row's menu, Download with its formats. The row reads back its current value, so the menu doubles as a summary of how the page is set, and opens a second menu to change it.

When to use

  • Use a nested menu for a setting or an action with a handful of choices, inside a menu that has other rows.
  • Use a section heading in the menu instead when the choices are few and the menu is short: a row's menu can list its statuses under “Change status” (see Popover).
  • Use a picker (a popover with a find box, or the picker sheet) for a long list of records: clients, people, items.

Anatomy

  1. Icon. The setting's, in the menu's icon column, so labels line up with the rows around it.
  2. Label. What the setting is.
  3. Value. What it's set to now, in the secondary ink.
  4. Chevron. Says there's a menu beyond it.

On a desktop

  • Where it opens. Beside the menu, on the side with room, its first item level with its row. With no room on either side, below the row (or above it near the bottom of the window).
  • A click opens it. A mouse resting on the row opens it after 120ms; resting on a row without one closes it after 200ms, so a pointer crossing on its way to the nested menu doesn't lose it.
  • The row stays lit while its menu is open. The nested menu is as wide as its longest option, so the check sits close to the chosen one.

On phones

On phones every menu is a bottom sheet, with no room beside it, so the row drills in instead:

  • The row's items replace the sheet's rows (every other row goes), and the sheet eases to its new height.
  • A header on top is the way back: Back on the left, the menu's name in the middle, a rule below. The whole header taps back.
  • A pick acts and closes the sheet. A tap outside, or Escape, closes it from either level.
  • Rows are a touch target tall (44).

Keyboard

KeyOn a row with a nested menuIn the nested menu
ArrowRight, Enter, SpaceGoes in, focus on the checked item or the firstEnter and Space pick
ArrowLeftComes back out, focus on the row
EscapeCloses the menu, focus back on its buttonComes back out, focus on the row; again, closes the menu
ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Home, EndMove between a menu's items, skipping disabled and hidden ones

Defined once

The row, where its menu sits, the phone sheet's drill, the mouse and the keys are the shell's (BolsterShell.nestedMenus, D126). A page keeps what's in each menu and what a pick does. The lists' display menu and row menus and the pipeline's display settings use it. Before it moved up, the copies had drifted: one page's arrow keys landed on a phone-only row on desktops, a mouse could drill a phone-width sheet, and one sheet snapped to its new height while another eased.

Accessibility

  • The row is a menuitem with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded while its menu is open, and aria-controls naming it.
  • The nested menu is a role="menu" labelled with the row's name. A setting's options are menuitemradio with aria-checked; the check is drawn as well, never only the state.
  • Focus always lands on a real item: going in, on the checked one; coming out, on the row.

Code

<div class="submenu-row">
  <button type="button" class="menu-item submenu-trigger" role="menuitem" data-sub="height"
    aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="pop-sub-height">
    <span class="menu-icon">…</span><span class="submenu-label">Row height</span><span class="submenu-value">Comfortable</span>…chevron…
  </button>
  <div class="pop submenu" id="pop-sub-height" popover role="menu" aria-label="Row height">
    <button type="button" class="menu-item submenu-back" data-sub-back="height" aria-label="Back">…</button>
    <button type="button" class="menu-item" role="menuitemradio" aria-checked="true">Comfortable…</button>
    <button type="button" class="menu-item" role="menuitemradio" aria-checked="false">Compact</button>
  </div>
</div>

// the shell wires it; the page draws its rows with row() and keeps what a pick does
const nest = BolsterShell.nestedMenus(pop);
nest.row("height", "Row height", "line-height", "Comfortable", itemsHtml);
