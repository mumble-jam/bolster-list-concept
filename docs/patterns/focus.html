Focus and keyboard

One focus ring, shown for the keyboard only. Focus goes into what opens and comes back to what opened it, busy buttons keep their place, and nothing that updates moves the page or the focus from under the person.

Stabletokens.cssbase.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-10-26

Everything in Bolster can be done from the keyboard, and the keyboard is never lost: the ring shows where it is, overlays take it in and give it back, and nothing that redraws while someone works takes it away.

One focus ring

  • For the keyboard only. The ring shows on :focus-visible, so a mouse click doesn't leave a ring behind, and Tab always does.
  • One ring everywhere: 2px in the focus blue (blue print 500 in light, 300 in dark), 2px off the edge. Blue means focus, selection and links in the system; it's never a button colour.
  • Fields draw it on themselves: the edge turns blue with a 1px ring inside it, no outer outline, so a field in a tight row doesn't overlap its neighbours.
  • On the dark top bar the search field takes a 1px ring with a soft halo; a 2px ring shouted on the near-black bar.
  • Never animated. It's there the moment focus arrives.

Where focus goes

Whatever opens takes focus; whatever closes gives it back to what opened it (I11). If that element is gone (the row was archived from its record), focus goes to the list's heading. Never to the page's body.

Focus never waits for motion: it's in a dialog, a menu or a sheet the moment it opens, and the keys work at once (I22, proposed).

Keys that work everywhere

KeyDoes
Tab, Shift+TabMoves between controls; inside a dialog, cycles within it.
Enter, SpaceActivates a button; Space toggles a checkbox. Enter follows a link.
EscapeCloses the innermost thing open: a nested menu, then its menu, a popover, a sheet, a dialog. In a field with words, it empties it first. With rows ticked and nothing open, it clears the selection. While dragging, it puts the thing back.
ArrowsMove within a group that's one tab stop: a menu's items, a segmented control, tabs, the calendar's days.
Home, EndJump to the first or last item of a menu, tabs, or a week in the calendar.
⌘K, Ctrl+K, /Focus the top bar's search from anywhere (/ only where the page has no search of its own).

Every pointer-only gesture has a keyboard way: a drag is Move in a summary (see Drag and drop), a hover is focus (a tooltip shows on focus at once).

Busy buttons

While an action runs, its button shows a spinner and a progressive label (“Saving…”), sets aria-busy="true" and ignores clicks. It stays enabled for screen readers, so they don't lose their place, and it keeps its width, so nothing beside it moves (I12).

The reader is never moved

Anything that updates while the person acts elsewhere changes its values, never its layout (I13). A redraw keeps the scroll position and the focus: a sticky header that's redrawn takes focus without scrolling (focus({ preventScroll: true })), and content that shrinks doesn't pull the page out from under the pointer. Sorting a column keeps the focus on its header, and the page where it was. See Feedback and undo for how states keep their shape.

Still open in the list concept: the chips row pushes the table down when the first filter is added, and filtering to nothing collapses the table under the pointer.

Usage

  • Give a heading that takes focus tabindex="-1", so it can be focused without joining the tab order.
  • When an action removes the element that has focus, move focus on purpose, to the next sensible place, before the element goes.
  • Hide what's out of sight from focus too: a folded menu, a closed sheet and a drawer pushed aside are inert or hidden, so Tab never lands under something.

What it replaces

  • Buttons with outline: none and no focus state, on all five of the as-is severities.
  • Overlays that dropped focus on the page's body when they closed, and a select sheet Escape couldn't close.
  • A list that scrolled the page when a sorted header took focus.
