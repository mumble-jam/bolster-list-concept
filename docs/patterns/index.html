Patterns

How things behave across components and pages: scrolling, opening, filtering, selecting, picking, giving feedback, moving focus and finding your way. Each one is a contract, written once and inherited by every page.

A component is a thing on the screen. A pattern is how several of them behave together, the same way on every page: what a row click does, where focus goes when a sheet closes, what stays on screen as you scroll, how a change says it happened and how it's taken back.

Where a pattern livesWhat it holds
The contractAn I-entry in Interaction contracts: the trigger, what moves, where focus goes, the keys, how it closes, and the as-is behaviour it replaces. The decisions behind it are D-entries in the Decision log.
The code, onceThe system's CSS (system/patterns/list-page.css and the components), and the app shell's few scripts (concepts/shared/shell.js): what hides and sticks as you scroll, nested menus, filter chips, the search button, the week's first day, toasts.
A pagePasses only what's its own: where its content starts, what's in its menus, what a pick does. It never copies a behaviour. When a second page needs something a first page has, it moves up into the shell or the system first.

That last rule is why the pages agree. When the to-do list copied the list's scrolling script, its sticky panel slid under the top bar; when nested menus were copied page to page, one page's arrow keys landed on a hidden row. Defined once, a fix reaches every page at the same time (D73, D126).

A pattern marked trying out is built in the concepts and being tested; a draft is built on one or two pages and waits for the product designer's approval before it's written as a rule for all of them.

  • Page scrollingStableThe page is the one scroller: headers stick under the bars, wide parts scroll sideways only, their scrollbar where they sit (D141).
  • Opening a recordStableA row click peeks or opens; a row's next step leads its record, never a button on the row.
  • Filtering and searchStableCards as work queues, chips for what's set, Add filter for the rest, search in the toolbar.
  • Bulk actionsStableCheckboxes only where there are bulk actions; the bar counts and acts, select mode on phones.
  • Picking datesStablePresets first, then a range on two months; weeks start on Monday everywhere.
  • Nested menusStableA row that reads its value and opens a second level beside it, or drills in on phones.
  • Feedback and undoStableSay what happened where it happened; offer Undo instead of asking first, where it can be undone.
  • Focus and keyboardStableOne focus ring, focus returned where it came from, busy buttons and live areas that keep their shape.
  • Navigation at each widthTrying outThe menu pinned, folded or a drawer; the phone's bars that go as you scroll.
  • Global searchTrying outOne field in the top bar finds any record by type; it finds, it doesn't create.
  • Drag and dropDraftMoving tasks, cards and bars: where it will land, what a drop asks, and the keyboard's way.
  • Forms and validationTrying outForms that fill themselves (D140): start from what's there, every value marked with where it came from, the record a page whose values change in place; underneath, a form done well (D134) for every field that remains: one column, labels above, errors in words, one Save.
  • Destructive actionsNeeds workWhen to ask first, when to offer Undo, and how deleting for good reads.
  • Permissions and read-onlyNot built yetWhat people see when they can't do something: hidden, disabled with a reason, or read-only.
  • Errors and offlineNot built yetA page that can't load, a link to nothing, a lost connection on a job site.
  • First use and onboardingNot built yetA company's first day: empty pages that teach, and the getting-started checklist.
  • Export and printNot built yetTaking a list or a record elsewhere: CSV, PDF, print.
