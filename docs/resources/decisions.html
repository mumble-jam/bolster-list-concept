Decision log

Every token, component and behaviour decision in the system, numbered, with its reason, what it replaces and its status. Read straight from docs/decisions.md.

docs/decisions.mdUpdated 2026-10-29

Each D-entry names what it replaces in the live product and in the React prototype. Decided: agreed, or clearly implied. Trying out: built and in use, still being judged. Proposed: open for review. Superseded: replaced by a later entry, kept so the reasoning isn't lost.

D1Token structure follows the prototype

Decided#
  • Two layers: primitives (the palette) and semantic tokens (the API). Components use semantic tokens only; grep in system/components/ finds no primitive references.
  • Semantic names are the prototype's: surface / content / line / action / feedback / control, read as <category>-<role>[-<state>]. Same variable names as proto theme.css, so the React prototype and this CSS read the same variables.
  • Primitive values come from the prototype (which mirrors Figma), not from Bolster's live CSS. Where they differ, the live value is drift:

  • Added beyond the prototype: status-* (D12), avatar-* (D13), surface-chrome* and content-on-chrome* (the dark top bar and rail, which stay dark in both themes), layers (D9), control heights, shell sizes, content widths and type roles (D6).

D24 px spacing grid

Decided#
  • Every layout value is a multiple of 4: --space-1 (4) to --space-20 (80). --space-0-5 (2px) exists for optical nudges inside controls (segmented control inset), never for layout.
  • As-is: a 2px grid, with 6px (4,084 uses), 10px (272) and 14px (631) as common values.
  • Migration: 6 → 4 or 8 (8 for gaps between items, 4 inside compact controls); 10 → 8 or 12; 14 → 12 or 16. Control heights and line heights are on the grid too (D5, D6).

D3Warm ink, cool surfaces and lines

Superseded#
  • Text and icons use the warm pitch-black ramp; surfaces and borders use the cool cool-gray ramp. This is the prototype's split.
  • The audit's warm/cool finding was about accidents: Tailwind's gray-200 next to Bolster's #e5e3e0 on the same kind of element. A systematic split is different: every border is cool, every text is warm.
  • Alternative: keep lines warm like today's product (pitch-black-200/300). One token swap (--color-line-*) changes it everywhere. Worth a side-by-side before committing.

D5Button and control sizes on one height scale

Decided#
  • --control-height-sm/md/lg = 32 / 40 / 48, plus button xl 56 for field-app hero actions.
  • Button sizes are renamed to match: proto xs/sm/md/lg (32/40/48/56) → sm/md/lg/xl. Default is md (40), which is the prototype's default in pixels.
  • As-is: small buttons ranged 18–62px, large 36–64px. Fields were 48px next to 40px selects.
  • Small buttons get a 44px touch area on coarse pointers without drawing bigger.

D6Type roles, body at 14/20

Decided#
  • Every text style is one of 11 roles in utilities.css (.text-title-page, .text-body, .text-label, .text-caption, .text-amount-hero…). Line heights are on the 4px grid (16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40).
  • Body is 14/20 (proto default text-sm). As-is body was split across 12, 13, 14, 15 and 16px, with 13px the most common.
  • Retired sizes: 13, 15, and the fractional rem leftovers (12.8, 14.4, 12.5, 13.5, 19.2). 83 rendered type styles become 11 roles.
  • Weights 400 / 500 / 700 only. 600 (13 uses) and 300 (69 uses, including the count badge) are gone; Cera Pro ships no 600.
  • Space Grotesk for titles only (font-display). As-is it also rendered 13px body text 572 times.

D7Form controls: native inputs, visible borders, errors on the field

Decided#
  • Checkbox, radio, toggle (role="switch") and segmented control are native inputs, restyled. Keyboard, forms and screen readers work with no JS. The prototype used <button role="checkbox"> with JS.
  • Field border line-input = pitch-black-500, 4.0:1 on white. Clears the 3:1 non-text minimum. As-is was #9e979b (2.9:1); the prototype's pitch-black-200 was 1.4:1.
  • Errors colour the border and the helper text (the prototype coloured only the helper).
  • One "on" colour for checked controls: control-selected (black; light in dark). As-is had black, brand blue and blue-400 depending on the class.
  • Toggle is 44 × 24 with a 20px square knob (on grid); proto was 45 × 22.
  • Selects: native <select> for short lists. Long, searchable lists open the picker sheet (D16), replacing the as-is bottom-right sheet built on a tertiary button.

D8One focus ring

Decided#
  • :focus-visible everywhere: 2px line-focus (blue-print-500; blue-print-300 in dark), 2px offset. Inputs draw it as a blue border plus 1px inner ring.
  • As-is: no button severity had a visible focus state (outline-none with a transparent outline, measured on all five severities).

D9Layer scale

Decided#

--z-raised 10, sticky 100, shell 200, scrim 300, modal 400, popover 500, toast 600, tooltip 700. Dialogs and menus use the top layer (<dialog>, popover), so they don't need z-index at all. As-is rendered 22 values, up to 2147483647.

D10Motion

Decided#
  • --duration-fast 120ms (hovers, state flips), --duration-medium 240ms (panels, dialogs). Nothing slower. Transitions name their properties, never all.
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns motion off globally (base.css).
  • As-is: 16 rendered transitions, including the 1s width animation on the rail.

D11Three text levels, all AA

Decided#
  • content-primary (black), content-secondary (pitch-black-700), content-tertiary (pitch-black-600). All clear 4.5:1 on white, canvas, sidebar and selected rows.
  • As-is: seven greys for text plus pure black.
  • Changed from proto: secondary 600 → 700 and tertiary 400 → 600, because proto tertiary (placeholder) was 2.9:1.

D12Job status tokens

Decided#
  • status-{lead, draft, pending, booked, in-progress, active, outstanding}-{surface, content, dot}. Status badges always show a dot.
  • "Booked" moves from Tailwind lime (#a3e635) to Bolster green (green-100/800/500).
  • All pairs pass 4.5:1 in both themes.

D13Avatar palette

Decided#

Eight AA-safe backgrounds for white initials (avatar-1…8), chosen by a stable hash of the person's id. Always round. As-is generated colours included #2ffb1a with white text (fails).

D14Dark theme

Proposed#
  • Semantic colours are written once with light-dark(). The OS preference applies by default, and data-theme="light|dark" on <html> forces a theme. Shadows deepen in dark via a small override.
  • The top bar and rail (surface-chrome) stay dark in both themes.
  • Primary flips to yellow with black text. Accent and danger use deeper steps so white text still passes.
  • Five dark-only primitives (pitch-black-night-900/950, cement-night-700/800/900).
  • Status: contrast-checked (npm run contrast), not yet designed in Figma. Bolster has no dark theme today (its dark: utilities are unused).

D15Shell and breakpoints

Decided#
  • ≥1024: rail (60) + section sidebar (300) + content. 768–1023: rail + content; the sidebar opens as an overlay from the top bar. <768: bottom tab bar, no rail.
  • As-is kept the sidebar open at 768, squeezing forms to 488px with three-line labels.
  • Rail items are named by tooltips (and aria-labelledby), not by a 1s hover expansion. Superseded by D25: the rail expands on hover, as the product does.
  • Content widths: --content-max-width 1280, -wide 1500 (dashboards), -reading 768 (forms, documents).
  • The action bar sits above the bottom tab bar on phones. As-is, the tab bar covered the proposal's Approve button.
  • Breakpoints stay Tailwind's (640/768/1024/1280) so the prototype's utilities keep working.
  • <768, More (and the top bar's menu button) opens every first-level item, the rail's list, since phones have no rail; an item with its own items drills in to them with a way back. Only 768–1023, where the rail is there, does the menu button open the current section's items.

D16Overlays use the platform

Decided#
  • Dialogs are native <dialog> with showModal(): Escape, focus move, focus trap, focus return and inert background come free. Menus use popover, with CSS anchor positioning where supported. Tooltips are CSS only.
  • Fixes the as-is overlays: the select sheet ignored Escape and had no dialog role, focus fell to <body> after closing, and no trigger exposed aria-expanded.
  • Three dialog variants: centred (default), sheet (picker; bottom sheet on phones), workspace (the job modal). Contracts in docs/interactions.md.

D17Lists are tables with row links

Decided#
  • <table class="data-table">, where each row's name is a link stretched over the row. One click or Enter opens the row, and screen readers list the links.
  • As-is rows were divs with no role or tabindex: a single click selected, a double click opened, and keyboard users couldn't do either.
  • Selection is an explicit checkbox; the bulk bar appears when anything is selected.
  • Filter cards are a radio group with a tinted selected state. As-is they were buttons acting as tabs, with the selected one filled solid blue.

D18Radius

Decided#

4px default (--radius-sm) for buttons, fields, badges-square and filter cards, replacing the live .2rem (3.2px). 8px for menus, 12px for cards and dialogs, 16px for the workspace. As-is had 12 uniform radii rendered, 37 declared.

D19No Tailwind default palette

Decided#

System CSS references only its own tokens. The live product compiles about 20 Tailwind stock colour families alongside its own. The prototype generates utilities from theme.css, which already excludes them.

D20Delivery: plain CSS, layered, low specificity

Decided#
  • @layer tokens, base, components, utilities. Components use :where() and data attributes (data-variant, data-size, data-state for specimens only), so any single class overrides them.
  • system/bolster.css imports everything. package.json exports bolster-ds/system/*, so the Vite prototype can npm i ../Bolster and import "bolster-ds/system/bolster.css".
  • Cera Pro isn't shipped (licensed). base.css uses local(); the consuming app declares its own @font-face. Space Grotesk comes from Google Fonts.

D21Accent blue

Decided#

action-accent = blue-print-600 (blue-print-500 in dark) for links, selection, the selected filter card and count badges. White text on it passes in both themes. As-is used four blues (#3969ff, #084bff, #003fe9, #0039d2) plus Tailwind's #2563eb and #4f46e5.

D22Toasts

Decided#

One live region per page. Success and info leave after 5s, paused on hover and focus. Errors use role="alert", stay until dismissed and say what to do next. As-is error copy ("private object… accessable") is rewritten in the specimen.

D23Icons: Font Awesome 6 Pro

Decided#

The product's own set: Light for navigation and controls, Regular for search and help, as in the live app. Chosen by the product designer on 2026-10-08. The system CSS stays icon-agnostic (components size any svg). Concepts draw icons from concepts/shared/icons.js, a subset generated by scripts/build-icons.mjs from the Pro packages. The Font Awesome token is never stored in this repo; the packages are installed outside it. Until they are, the subset holds Lucide stand-ins under the same names.

D24Shell matches the product's measurements

Decided#

The top bar, rail and section sidebar take the live app's sizes and colours (measured on next.bolsterbuilt.com, Oct 2026), at the product designer's request:

  • Top bar 60; logo 28 in a rail-wide cell; company switcher 32 tall, 14px medium; global search 36 × up to 672, 16px; round 36px icon buttons with 20px icons; avatar 32.
  • Rail 60 with 12/8 padding; 44px items stacked without gaps, 8px radius, icons 16 tall in a 20px-wide box (Font Awesome's fixed width; first logged here as 20px, corrected in D25); pitch-black-300 icons, a light white overlay on hover, pitch-black-700 for the current one.
  • Section sidebar 300 on cement-100; a 64px header with the section name (Space Grotesk 24) over a rule; 36px items with 2px between them, 14px medium labels, 18px icons in a 28px column; the current item is white with a soft shadow; settings groups use 12px medium labels.
  • One deliberate difference: the sidebar's right edge uses line-sidebar (cement-400), not the product's darker cement-500, which reads as a yellow stroke next to the content.

New tokens: --shell-top-control-height (36), --shell-rail-item-size (44), --shell-sidebar-header-height (64), --shell-nav-item-height (36). --color-surface-sidebar moved to cement-100, its hover to cement-200, --color-content-on-chrome-muted to pitch-black-300, --color-surface-chrome-hover to an 8% white overlay. Contrast check passes.

D25The rail expands on hover, and can be pinned

Decided#

Reverses the D15 bullet on tooltips, at the product designer's request; it follows the product (next.bolsterbuilt.com, Oct 2026):

  • On hover the rail widens from 60 to 232 over the page, with a shadow, and shows each item's label (14px medium, starting 52 from the edge). The page doesn't move.
  • It opens after a 150ms pause, so crossing the rail on the way to the sidebar doesn't flash it, and closes 100ms after the pointer leaves. The width animates over 240ms, eased out. Keyboard focus opens it straight away; Escape closes it.
  • Bottom of the rail: Messages, Files, Settings, Switch company, then a 28px rule (192 when expanded) and Pin sidebar with the product's own panel-and-chevron icon. Pinned, the rail takes 232 in the layout and the label reads "Unpin sidebar". Pinning applies from 1024 up.
  • Icons are 16 tall in a 20px-wide box, as Font Awesome's fixed width draws them (D24 had 20).
  • No "What's new" rocket: in the product that slot is a loading indicator, not a destination.
  • Labels are real text in the links, so the tooltips are gone and the links have names without aria-labelledby.

--shell-rail-expanded-width moves from 240 to 232. Classes: .rail[data-expanded], .app-shell[data-rail-pinned], .rail-label, .rail-separator. Behaviour in I9.

D26The section sidebar fits its longest item plus 48

Decided#

At the product designer's request, the section sidebar narrows from the product's 300 to 256, so the page gets the width back:

  • The longest item in any section is "Leads and customers" (Customers). Measured in Cera Pro 14px medium, its item is 190 wide: 8 padding, a 28px icon column, an 8px gap, 138 of label, 8 padding.
  • 190, plus 48 of free room after it, plus the sidebar's 8 + 8 padding and its 1px rule, makes 255, rounded to 256 (16rem). The longest label then ends 57 short of the current item's highlight (48 plus the item's own 8 padding, plus the pixel from rounding).
  • One width for every section, so the page doesn't shift when you move between them. Settings' longest ("Presentation themes", 187) and the section titles fit inside it.
  • Re-measure if a longer label is added; the token comment says where the number comes from.

--shell-sidebar-width moves from 300 to 256. Overrides the sidebar width in D24.

D27Explanations use the screen's own words

Decided#

Copy that explains what's on screen (an info popover, a helper line, an empty state, a tooltip) names things exactly as the screen labels them: the same card, status, column and filter names, in the same case. It never brings in a synonym ("lost" for Declined, "everything invoiced" for three statuses). Where it needs a term the screen doesn't show (won, decided, invoiced), it defines it once, in the screen's labels ("Won is Booked plus In progress"), and then uses it the same way everywhere, including in the card's own figures ("62 of 85 decided", "Average won job").

  • On-screen terms stand out in the explanation: a status carries its mark or dot, as on its card; any other label is set in medium weight.
  • Where a list borrows another list's terms (a customer's lifetime value adds up their jobs), it uses that list's labels as they appear there.
  • In the data list concept, explanations are written with {status} for a status and [Label] for any other on-screen label; data-list.js draws them.

Came from the jobs' Win rate note, which said "won", "declined" and "pending" in its own words while the cards beside it said Booked, In progress and Declined.

D28The page title is the menu item's name

Decided#

A page's title in its header is the label of the menu item that opens it, word for word: Job list, not Jobs; Leads and customers, not Customers. The section's name (Jobs) belongs to the section sidebar's heading, so it already sits right above.

  • The browser tab follows the same title (Job list · Bolster).
  • One source: pages ask the shell for the current item's label (BolsterShell.label(id) in the concepts) and never type their own. Renaming a menu item renames its page.
  • Copy that needs a plural noun ("Jobs you create show up here") uses the list's noun, not the title.
  • Pages that aren't menu items (a job, an estimate) take the record's name instead.

Came from the jobs list, whose header said Jobs while the selected menu item said Job list.

D29On phones, Create floats above the tab bar

Decided#

On phones the page's main create action ("+ Create job") leaves the page header and floats bottom right, just above the tab bar, where the thumb already is. The top bar and page header stay as they are.

  • Labelled at rest, so it reads as the page's main action, not a mystery +.
  • While you scroll down (the chrome slides away) it shrinks to a 56 px +, so it covers less of the list; it grows back with the chrome. The change is one movement with the bars (13 Oct, your ask): 300 ms, the bars' easing; the button narrows from the left while its right edge holds, the label slides into the right edge and dissolves through a soft edge (its last 16 px fade, about a fifth of the label; your call, so it isn't cut at full strength), and the + grows into the 56 px square. Back, the label comes out of the same soft edge, which sharpens once the button is open. 56 tall in both states (14 Oct, your call), so only the width moves. Reduced motion swaps without moving. The label stays the button's accessible name when collapsed.
  • While rows are ticked it steps aside and the bulk bar takes its place: one thing at the bottom at a time.
  • The list gets room after its last row, so the button never sits on it.
  • Tablet and desktop keep Create in the page header.

Came from the phone header research (Figma, research page, frames D). A first version also folded the page header into a one-bar top bar; that part was reverted, and only the floating Create came back.

D30A row click either peeks or opens

Decided#

Every list says what clicking a row does, and it's one of two:

  • Peek: the record opens beside the list from 1280 up, and as a side sheet below that. The list keeps its place, and the peek has a button to the full record. For lists people triage.
  • Open: the row goes straight to the record itself, as a page or a full-screen workspace (I7). For lists people work in.

Checkbox clicks never open, and the row's name stays a real link either way (D17). The jobs list peeks in the concept; Bolster may choose open for jobs later. A screenshot can't show which one a page uses, so the page survey always asks (rule L-row-click).

D31Pages are described, and the rules are written once

Proposed#

A page is built from a page description (data: columns and their types, statuses, cards, filters, actions, what a click does), not drawn by hand. Lists first: system/recipes/list/.

  • schema.json is the description's shape; rules.json the rules, with the column types and the survey's questions; check.mjs fails a description that breaks a rule.
  • The same files feed the back office (system/specimens/list-recipe.html, generated by scripts/build-rules.mjs) and the front office (the bolster-page skill, which turns a screenshot and a short survey into a description). Neither keeps its own copy, so they can't drift.
  • Each rule is decided (it has a D- or I-entry) or proposed (a suggestion); the front office uses both and names the proposed ones in its hand-over.
  • The concept's four lists are written as descriptions in system/recipes/list/concept/.
  • The renderer: concepts/data-list/recipe.js compiles a description into the list config the concept draws, with sample rows made from each column's type. ?from=description builds the four lists that way; ?d=<path> opens any description (a dry run's, a skill's output). What it can't draw (custom figures in cards, an insight) it names in a note on the page, never fakes.
  • Next: custom figures as named, reusable measures; the peek's history from the status stages. (The Next step column was dropped: D43.)

D32Phone rows have four corners and an unbroken name

Decided#

Direction C of the tables-on-phones research (Figma, “Tables on phones · research”). Below 768 a row is an item: the id and a date on an overline top left (12 px, secondary), the key figure top right (16 px medium, tabular), the name across the full width (16/22 medium, wrapping to two lines, never cut on the first), then where (address, job or unit) bottom left at 14/20 and the status bottom right. The names were cut on 24 of 25 job rows before; now about 1 in 5 wraps.

  • Rows lead with a name (L-name-first, now decided): invoices show the customer, with the number on the overline (and on the detail line on wider screens). Jobs keep “Customer · job”.
  • The research's 13 px overline is 12 here: the system has no 13, and nothing goes below 12.
  • Selecting is a mode on phones (I6): Select in the page header, or a long press.
  • Lists say what goes on the overline and under the name (phone in the list config); a list built from a description gets its id field and top date on the overline and the name's detail, less the id, underneath. Without either, the top date takes the overline.
  • Owner and created date live in the record. Still open: the opt-in table view (direction E), and whether the overline's date should be last activity, created or scheduled.

D33The status quo: warm light, cool dark, and the list page in the system

Decided#

What the list concept settled is now the system, to build on until it changes. It may still change a lot; until then new work starts from system/, and anything that disagrees with it is changed there first.

  • Light is warm, dark is cool. In light, cement neutrals on a paper canvas (cement-200), white cards with cement edges, cement hovers and tints; text, inputs and the dark chrome keep Bolster's ink, and selection stays blue. In dark, Bolster's pitch-black greys (what the concept called the cool canvas). The concept's Colour switch is gone. Settles D3.
  • New tokens: line-card (cards, menus and popovers), surface-current (the row whose record is open), surface-hover-control (hover on white: menus, the table card's header), content-section (menu section labels), highlight-search, feedback-danger-on-inverse, the status mark colours (status-draft|pending|in-progress-mark), the secondary and ghost button colours, shadow-tint and shadow-popover. In progress is blue. The scrim is warm in light, for sheets and dialogs alike.
  • Into system/components/: status marks (badge.css); the tactile buttons, every button with 8px corners and tertiary gone (button.css); the cards' body (card.css; data-cards="flat" turns it off); warm menus (popover.css); and new files for the toolbar, chips, summary cards, popovers and bottom sheets, the floating bar, the record, and the states.   • Moved as they were. Turning these into :where() selectors, as the older components are, is a later tidy-up.
  • Still in the concept: its tooling (controls, tuners, the About dialog, the recipe note) and the grid on the page, which is still being tried. The Bolster shell's extra parts stay in concepts/shared/.
  • Back office: the catalog has a status quo note, a Page types section (the list page, the list recipe) and a page for each list part; Badge and Button show the marks and the tactile roles.

D34The bulk bar: a count chip and icons on desktop; the tab bar's place on phones

Decided#

From the bulk actions research, round two (Figma, “Bulk actions bar · research”, 07 A and D).

  • Desktop (A). Same place, same dark capsule; the parts change. The count is a chip that holds its own ×, and Escape clears the selection from anywhere in the page (unless a menu, a dialog or a text field takes it first), so the bar's end is free. Select all is a quiet underlined link; with everything ticked the chip says “All 132 selected” and the link turns into Unselect all. Actions carry the product's 14 px icons beside their labels; Change status wears the status icon.
