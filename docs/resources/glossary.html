Glossary

The words Bolster and this system use, and what each means here: the trade's records, the statuses they move through, and the system's own terms.

Bolster's words come first: the system uses the product's names for things, in the product's case, and explanations on screen use the screen's own words (D27). Where a word means one thing in Bolster and another in the system (a catalog, a stage), both are here.

The trade

Appointment
A sales appointment: a visit or a call booked with a customer, at a time, with someone from the team. Its type sets its length (an estimate visit, a measure, a project consultation). One of the calendar's two kinds of booking; the other is an event.
Assembly
A part of an estimate that holds items, labour and selections, nested to any depth, with its category (“Roofing – Shingle”). Its cost and price add up what's in it.
AutoCost
Bolster's tag on an item whose price is kept up to date from the supplier.
Bid request (RFQ)
One trade of a job (its budget items grouped by trade: plumbing fixtures and rough-in under Plumbing) sent to vendors to price. Reviewed and sent, then waiting while the vendors price each item through a link, until their bids are back. Bolster's address for them is ~/rfqs.
Catalog
The company's items (Bolster's Items, ~/items): what it sells and buys, each with its photo, category and vendor.
Crew
The company's own people doing the work, as against subcontractors. The calendar's Crew view lays out each person's week; the timeline's Done by tells our crew from subcontractors.
Customer, client
The person or household the work is for. Bolster's lists say customer (Customers: Lead, Active, Past); its jobs and documents say client. A lead is a customer with no job yet.
Estimate
What a job will cost: a tree of assemblies, items, labour and selections, priced with markup and tax up to the job's total. Creating a job makes a draft estimate; sent to the client, it's the proposal.
Event
On the calendar, a booking that isn't a sales appointment: a title, a time or all day, whose calendar it's on, guests, a location.
Expense
Money a job costs that the business pays out: a vendor's bill or a receipt for something bought, added by the team or submitted by the vendor, unpaid until someone marks it paid.
Invoice
What the client is asked to pay: a record of its own, with its own window. A progress invoice while the job is underway; the final one once the work is done.
Item
Something the company sells or buys: in the catalog, and as a line of an estimate.
Job
Bolster's centre: the work for a client, with its estimate, schedule, money, files and people. A job opens into its workspace.
Labor and materials
The two kinds of thing an order, an item or a line holds, defined once for every page (system/model/kinds.json): a hard hat for labour, a box for materials, named together in that order. Bolster's labels spell it “Labor”.
Lead
A customer's record before there's a job: a person and what they asked for. On the pipeline, Leads and Appointments hold people; every stage after them holds jobs.
Lost
On the pipeline, a lead, an appointment or a pending job marked lost with a reason: price, timing, went with someone else, no reply, not a fit. It can be reopened where it was.
Milestone
On the timeline, a date with no length, such as an inspection, drawn as a diamond.
Phase
A part of a job's plan that groups its stages: a job has phases, a phase has stages. On the calendar, a phase is a bar across its days.
Pipeline
Bolster's board (Jobs → Pipeline) from the first enquiry to the paid invoice: people, then jobs, then invoices, each stage a column.
Presentation
What the client sees: a proposal or an invoice as a long centred document, with a cover, a summary and sections, the decision in a bar that stays on screen. The same layout serves the public links clients open. Its look follows the company's presentation themes.
Proposal
The estimate as it goes to the client to approve. Bolster names the estimate's editor Proposal (~/estimate/:id); the job's Proposal presentation is what the client opens.
Selection
A choice on an estimate (a shingle's style and colour). The estimator makes some, marked with a lock; the customer makes the rest when they open the proposal.
Stage (pipeline)
A column of the pipeline: Leads, Appointments, Pending, Booked, In progress, Getting paid, and Lost.
Stage (timeline)
A dated piece of a job's work, under a phase, measured in working days. It can wait for another stage of the same job (a link, finish to start); its labour and materials fold under it.
Sub, subcontractor
A vendor who brings labour to a job, as against a supplier of materials. Bolster's page for them is Subs & vendors.
Task
A piece of work someone on the team has to do: one they wrote themselves, or a job's labour or material item turned into a task, with a due date, a priority and someone to do it, open until it's ticked off.
Trade
A kind of work: plumbing, roofing, insulation. A job's budget items are grouped by trade, and a vendor has the trades it works in.
Unscheduled
Jobs with no dates yet. They wait beside the calendar and the timeline, to be given a start.
Vendor
A company the business pays for work on its jobs: a subcontractor who brings labour, a supplier of materials, or both. Kept with how to reach it and the documents a job needs from it (insurance, licence, workers' comp); active while the business uses it.
Vendor order
An order the business places with a vendor for a job's labour, materials or both: written, sent, approved by the vendor, then paid once their invoice is in.

Statuses

A status is where a record is in its life. The model lists a thing's statuses in order, each with the step that moves it on. Jobs' statuses have their own shapes, filling a quarter at a time as the job moves on; every other list shows a status by its colour alone (D49).

Draft
A job whose estimate hasn't been sent; an invoice not sent yet.
Pending
A job whose estimate is sent, awaiting the client's reply.
Booked
A job the client approved, work not started. Booked is won.
In progress
A job whose work is underway.
Declined
A job the client said no to. With Booked and In progress, it makes the jobs that are decided.
Lead
A customer with no job yet.
Active
A customer with open jobs; a vendor the business uses.
Past
A customer from past work, with no open jobs.
Outstanding
An invoice sent and not yet paid, not yet past its due date.
Overdue
Past its due date and not done: an invoice not paid, a task not completed. Nobody sets it; it's worked out from the date (D70).
Paid
An invoice, an expense or a vendor order that's settled. An ending.
Not sent, Awaiting approval, Approved
A vendor order's way to Paid: written, sent to the vendor, approved by them. Declined (by the vendor) and Cancelled are its other endings.
Not sent, Waiting, Bids back
A bid request's statuses: not out yet, out with vendors, every bid back.
To do, Completed
A task's statuses; Overdue sits between them when its due date passes.
Stage (of a status)
What kind of point in a life a status is: new, draft, waiting, won, underway, done, lost, late or neutral. The stage picks the status's colour, so the same stage looks the same on every list (L-status-stage).
Ending
A status where a record's life stops: Paid, Declined, Cancelled, Past. A summary counts endings for the last 30 days (D67).
Status tokens
The colours for the job statuses (--color-status-{lead, draft, pending, booked, in-progress, active, outstanding}-…), each a surface, content, dot and, where the shape carries it, a mark, all passing contrast in both themes (D12).

The system

Canvas
The page's own surface, paper in light, with a faint grid behind every page (D81). Cards sit on it.
Component
A part pages are built from (a button, a chip, the record), as one CSS file in system/components/ with classes and data attributes for its variants.
Concept
A whole page built in concepts/ from the system, with made-up data, to try a page type out. What settles in it moves into the system.
D-entry
A decision in docs/decisions.md, numbered (D12) and marked decided, trying out, proposed, superseded or dropped, with its reason. Every token and component decision has one.
Draft
A part built in a concept and working there, waiting for the product designer's approval before it moves into the system. Not to be confused with the Draft status.
I-entry
An interaction contract in docs/interactions.md (I6): how a thing behaves, to the key and the focus, at every width.
Intent
What one page is for, in a short file (pages/list/<nav>.intent.json): who comes, what they do there, the key figure, what they look at after the name. Written as needs, never as layout.
Kinds
Ideas that recur across things, defined once with their words and icons: labor and materials.
Model
What exists, with nothing about pages (system/model/<thing>.json): a thing's noun, how people name one, its fields, its lifecycle, its actions, whether it has its own window, each with its evidence. One per thing, shared by every page that shows it.
Next step
The action that moves a record on from its status (Send, Mark approved, Record payment), from its model's lifecycle. On a list it leads the record and the row's ··· menu, never a button on the row (D43); on the pipeline it's on the card.
Page description
What a built page is, as data (pages/list/<nav>.json): its columns, statuses, summary, filters, actions, what a click does. Written by the builder from the model and the intent, never edited.
Page type
A kind of page (a list, a board, a calendar) arranged from components and patterns, so every page of that kind is built the same way.
Pattern
How several components behave together across pages: filtering, bulk actions, opening records, page scrolling.
Quick look
The record beside the list, for a thing that has its own window: the row opens the window, and Quick look is in the row's ··· menu (D69).
Recipe
The rules a page type is built by, written once (system/recipes/list/): the rules, the description's schema, the builder and the checker. Lists have one.
Record
One row's details, opened from the list: beside it from 1280 wide, a side sheet below. Status, amount, activity, two actions and More, the fields.
Rule
One line of a recipe, named L-something (L-row-click, L-summary), with its reason, its sources and what enforces it.
Shell
The frame every page sits in: the dark top bar, the rail and menu, the tab bar on phones. What it does is defined once and inherited by every page.
Specimen
A component's workbench: a static page in system/specimens/ with every variant and state working on the system's own CSS. Each component's page here shows its specimen live, under “Every variant and state”.
Status quo
What the system is today, to build on until it changes: warm in light, cool in dark, the list page in the system (D33).
Summary card
A card above a list: a KPI (a figure that says how the list is doing) or a status of its lifecycle. A card that's a slice of rows filters the list when picked.
Survey
A built page's open questions as a form, each starting on what the page assumes until someone answers (D64).
Token
A named value in system/tokens.css, as a CSS custom property, light and dark. Primitives are the raw ramps (--blue-600); semantic tokens say what a value is for (--color-action-primary) and are the only ones components use.
Work queue
A summary card standing for rows someone has to act on (Not sent, Unpaid, Missing documents). D66 made every card one; D67 replaced that with two kinds of summary, KPIs and the lifecycle.
Workspace
A thing's own full-screen window, opened from a row or by Edit: the job's, with its own navigation, a vendor's, a job's bid workspace. Where people work on the thing (I7).
