Backlog

Everything the system doesn't have yet, everything it has but hasn't finished, and everything built in a concept and waiting for approval: one map, read from system/inventory.json.

Priorities are a first proposal, by how much of Bolster each thing blocks: high where a page type can't be designed without it, medium where pages work around it today, low where it waits for a page that isn't planned yet. Each thing not built yet has its own page: what it's for, where Bolster needs it, what it has to do and what has to be settled first.

Not built yet 31

Bolster needs these and the system doesn't have them. Each has a page saying what it's for, where Bolster needs it and what has to be settled.

  • File uploadHighComponentNot built yet

    Add photos and documents: a drop area, a button, progress, and the files as they land.

    • A drop area and a button (the camera on phones)
    • Each file's progress, its error with Try again, and Remove
    • Pictures as thumbnails as they finish (thumbnail.css)
  • Inline alertHighComponentNot built yet

    A message that stays on the page until it's dealt with: information, a warning, a success, an error.

    • Four tones on the feedback tokens, each with its icon (never colour alone)
    • A title, a line, at most two actions; dismissible only when nothing is lost
    • Errors announced (role="alert"), the rest polite
  • DashboardHighPage typeNot built yet

    How the business is doing: sales, jobs and money, with page-wide filters.

    • A layout grid at the wide content width (1500), cards that span it
    • Page-wide filters (dates, owner) that every widget follows
    • Each figure opens the list behind it, filtered
  • SettingsHighPage typeNot built yet

    The company's set-up: sections, forms and how they save.

    • The forms pattern: sections, field sizes, the save model
    • Settings' own navigation (the shell's Settings window, D89)
    • Dangerous settings (billing, integrations) apart and confirmed
  • Document and presentationHighPage typeNot built yet

    What the client sees: the proposal, the estimate and the invoice, on screen and as a PDF.

    • The contractor's logo and colour (presentation themes) inside the system's rules
    • Line items, totals, taxes and payment terms that read as a document, not a table
    • A print and PDF layout
  • Data visualisationMediumFoundationNot built yet

    Colours, marks and rules for charts: dashboards, reports and the summary cards' graphs.

    • A categorical palette (six to eight series) and a sequential one, as semantic tokens, checked for contrast against the card in light and dark
    • Marks: line weight, bar corners, the axis and grid lines, the tooltip
    • Money on axes as the content rules write it (no “3M”)
  • Split buttonMediumComponentNot built yet

    One main action, and a menu of its siblings beside it.

    • The main part is a button of any role; the arrow is a second button opening a menu (I3)
    • Both parts reachable by Tab, the arrow named (“More save options”) with a tooltip (D52)
    • Shares the button's sizes and corners; the seam between the two follows the nested-corner rule
  • Time fieldMediumComponentNot built yet

    A time of day, typed or picked, and a time range for appointments.

    • Typed (“9”, “9:30a”, “14:00”) and picked from a list in 15-minute steps
    • A range keeps its end after its start, and shows the duration
    • Times in the company's time zone, said once
  • ProgressMediumComponentNot built yet

    How far along something is: a job's work, an upload, the onboarding checklist.

    • A bar (determinate) with its value said in words for screen readers (progress or meter)
    • An indeterminate state that respects reduced motion
    • Colour from the meaning (success when done, the accent while going), never a rainbow
  • ChartsMediumComponentNot built yet

    Bar, line and donut charts on the system's data-visualisation rules.

    • Built on the data-visualisation foundation
    • A table of the same numbers, one click away, for screen readers and for exporting
    • A tooltip on the point, and the legend as filters
  • AccordionMediumComponentNot built yet

    Sections that fold: settings groups, an estimate's areas, long records.

    • <details> and <summary> (the platform), one or several open
    • The summary says what's inside when folded (a count, a total)
    • Motion on the motion tokens, none with reduced motion
  • BreadcrumbsMediumComponentNot built yet

    Where a record sits, and the way back: Jobs › Garage drywall › Estimate.

    • A nav with an ordered list, the current page last and not a link (aria-current)
    • On phones, only the parent as a back link
    • Long names shorten in the middle, never the current one
  • Permissions and read-onlyMediumPatternNot built yet

    What people see when they can't do something: hidden, disabled with a reason, or read-only.

    • Hide what someone will never be able to do; disable, with a tooltip saying why, what they could do in another state
    • Read-only records look read-only (values, not empty fields)
    • A locked record says why it's locked and who can unlock it
  • Errors and offlineMediumPatternNot built yet

    A page that can't load, a link to nothing, a lost connection on a job site.

    • Not found and no access as pages in the shell, each with a way on
    • Offline said once, in the shell; what was entered kept and sent later, or said plainly that it wasn't
    • Errors in the screen's own words, saying what to do next
  • MessagingMediumPage typeNot built yet

    Conversations with clients and vendors, tied to their jobs.

    • Threads by person and job, unread first
    • Bubbles, times, read state, attachments as thumbnails
    • A composer that keeps its draft
  • FilesMediumPage typeNot built yet

    Every photo and document, by job: browse, preview, share.

    • Grid and list views, by job and by type
    • A preview that pages through, with download and share
    • File upload
  • Field appMediumPage typeNot built yet

    Bolster on a phone on a job site: today's jobs, logs, tasks, photos, chat.

    • Large targets (the xl button, 56)
    • High contrast outdoors
    • Works with a weak signal (errors and offline)
  • Client and vendor portalMediumPage typeNot built yet

    The pages people outside the company see: a proposal to approve, an invoice to pay, a bid request to answer.

    • No app shell: the contractor's name and logo, then the one thing to do
    • Approve, sign, pay (payments stay with the provider's own form)
    • Phone first
  • Recipes for other page typesMediumToolingNot built yet

    The list recipe's way (rules, a builder, a checker) for boards, calendars and records.

    • A schema for the page's description, its rules, a builder and a checker, as the list's
  • Tokens for other toolsMediumToolingNot built yet

    The same tokens in Figma variables and as JSON, so design and code read one source.

    • A build from tokens.css to the design tokens format (W3C DTCG JSON), light and dark as modes
    • Figma variables from that JSON
    • Run in the same change as any token change
  • Versions and changelogMediumToolingNot built yet

    Numbered releases, a changelog people can read, and what breaks between them.

    • Semantic versions; a changelog grouped by added, changed, removed
    • Deprecations announced a release before removal
    • The documentation says which version it describes
  • Rich text editorLowComponentNot built yet

    Formatted text for what clients read: a proposal's cover, terms, emails.

    • CKEditor themed from the system's tokens (its --ck-* variables), or a smaller editor
    • A short toolbar: headings, bold, lists, links
    • What it writes reads the same in the client's document
  • SignatureLowComponentNot built yet

    A client signs a proposal or a change order: drawn, typed, and dated.

    • Draw with a finger or mouse, or type the name (Whisper, the script face, may come back here)
    • Name, date and what's being signed, said next to it
    • Clear and Done, keyboard reachable
  • StepperLowComponentNot built yet

    The steps of a flow and where you are in it: imports, onboarding, set-up.

    • An ordered list of steps: done, current, to come, each said in words
    • Going back keeps what was entered
    • On phones, “Step 2 of 4” and the step's name
  • NotificationsLowComponentNot built yet

    What happened while you were away: a client approved, a payment landed, a task is yours.

    • A count on the bell (the count badge), a panel of what's new, each opening its record
    • Read and unread, Mark all as read
    • Never the only way to learn something that needs action
  • First use and onboardingLowPatternNot built yet

    A company's first day: empty pages that teach, and the getting-started checklist.

    • First-use empty states apart from “no results”: what this is, the first action, maybe an import
    • The checklist: steps, done, skip, progress
    • Never in the way of someone who's done it before
  • Export and printLowPatternNot built yet

    Taking a list or a record elsewhere: CSV, PDF, print.

    • Export takes what's on screen: the filters, the sort and the columns
    • A print stylesheet for records and documents: no chrome, the document's own layout
  • Home and getting startedLowPage typeNot built yet

    Where a company lands: what needs them today, and on day one, the set-up checklist.

    • What needs me today: tasks, approvals, overdue invoices, today's schedule
    • The checklist while set-up isn't done
    • Built from summary cards and lists, not new components
  • ReportsLowPage typeNot built yet

    Tables and charts to answer one question at a time, exported.

    • A report's filters, its table (with totals), its chart
    • Export (CSV, PDF)
  • Wizard and importLowPage typeNot built yet

    A task in steps: importing clients or a catalog, setting up a flow.

    • The stepper, a step per screen, Back and Next
    • Mapping columns, a preview of what will be made, the errors before confirming
  • Sign inLowPage typeNot built yet

    Sign in, reset a password, accept an invitation.

    • One column, the brand, the fields, errors that don't say which part was wrong
    • Password managers and passkeys work

Needs work 8

In the system, but not fully addressed.

  • ContentHighFoundationNeeds work

    Sentence case, the screen's own words, and how values read: money, dates, ids.

    • No voice and tone guide: how Bolster sounds to a contractor, a client and a sub.
    • Formats are decided for what lists show (D59), not written down as one table: money, dates, times, ranges, addresses, phone numbers, quantities and units.
    • No guidance for error messages, confirmations and empty states' words beyond the examples in the components.
  • Empty, loading and errorHighComponentNeeds work

    What a part shows besides its content: skeletons in its shape while it loads (never a flash), a state when there's nothing yet or nothing matches, a banner when it failed.

    • The banner exists for errors only: there's no inline alert for information, a warning or a success that stays on the page (see Inline alert).
    • A first-use empty state (nothing has ever been made here) reads the same as “no results”; they need different words and actions.
  • Models of Bolster's thingsHighToolingNeeds work

    One model per thing (what it is, its fields, its statuses, its actions), shared by every page that shows it.

    • Five things have models (vendor orders, vendors, tasks, expenses, bid requests); the core ones don't: jobs, leads, clients, estimates, invoices, items. The concept's job and invoice lists are still written by hand.
    • Statuses beyond the job's have no colour map (see Colour).
  • Activity and commentsMediumComponentNeeds work

    A record's story, and the notes people leave on it.

    • The record shows Activity (what happened, D74) but nobody can write in it: no comment box, no mentions, no attachments on a note.
    • Messaging (Bolster's client and vendor messages) has no components: bubbles, the composer, read state.
  • Destructive actionsMediumPatternNeeds work

    When to ask first, when to offer Undo, and how deleting for good reads.

    • Undo is the default for what can be undone and a confirm dialog for what can't; there's no list of which Bolster actions are which (archive, delete, void an invoice, cancel a payment).
  • React componentsMediumToolingNeeds work

    The system as components for the React prototype, on the same CSS.

    • The prototype's kit (src/ui) is the first draft of the system, not built on system/ CSS: the two read the same token names but are separate code.
    • No component API is written down (props for each variant and size); the CSS's data attributes are the only contract.
  • Decisions that no longer match the CSSMediumToolingNeeds work

    Entries the code has moved past, and comments that cite the wrong decision: the record should say what the CSS does.

    • D12 says status badges always show a dot; badge.css draws none, following D61. D12 isn't marked.
    • D18's radius scale is the first one (see Shape). D6's role count and Space Grotesk's use are out of date (see Typography). D15's sidebar width is 300; the token is 256.
    • D10's 240ms ceiling and token-only timings are broken in four components (see Motion). D5's 44px touch area is only vertical.
    • D42 (the keycap) and D75 (Week and Day's hours scroll inside) were changed by D112 and D128 without being marked.
    • D16's anchor positioning isn't used; popovers are placed by script.
    • D41's worked example (the record's × at 18 Light) predates D84 (Regular).
    • Wrong citations in CSS headers: badge.css cites D28 for status marks (D49); summary-cards.css cites D20; toolbar.css and chip.css cite D19 and D21 for the table card's header and chips.
    • D20 says components use :where(); nine component files have none (D33 calls it a later tidy-up).
  • BrandLowFoundationNeeds work

    The mark, the wordmark, Level yellow, and the drawings the loader uses.

    • No rules for the logo: clear space, smallest size, what it may sit on.
    • Bolton, the mascot, and the loader's 16 drawings are in the concepts, not in the system.
    • The shell draws the logo with hard-coded colours, not the brand tokens, so its letters are pure black rather than Bolster's black.

Trying out 12

In use and working, still being judged.

  • MotionFoundationTrying out

    Two durations and two curves today; a fuller set by what moves, from the motion research, waiting for approval.

    • The motion tokens (D124) are in the motion lab, not yet in tokens.css; the system still has two durations and two curves.
    • Reduced motion turns everything off (D10); the research proposes fades instead (I22). One of the two has to go.
    • Components set their own timings instead of the tokens: the button's 30ms press and 150ms release, the summary card's 150ms, the record panel's and the table header's 300ms (past D10's 240ms ceiling), the table's 200ms, the skeleton's 1.4s loop.
    • Two tooltip waits: the CSS-only tooltip waits 150ms (I4), tooltip.js 300ms (D52).
  • ComboboxComponentTrying out

    Type to find a value in a long list, in the field itself: a client, a trade, the crew. Several values as chips; on phones, the picker sheet.

    • With nothing typed the list starts from the top; recent or frequent picks first isn't there.
    • No groups in the list (people and companies; materials and labor).
    • Which fields take free text and which only pick from a list is set per field (free); there's no rule for it yet.
    • The calendar's guests (a draft, D96) and the concepts' own pickers don't use it yet; they move to it when they're next touched.
  • Address fieldComponentTrying out

    A site's address, looked up as you type, its parts kept apart and each editable; the same fields by hand without a lookup.

    • The specimen lists 14 countries; a product lists them all (its own list, or the platform's names through Intl.DisplayNames).
    • No map pin, and no check that the address exists: the lookup's answer is taken as given.
    • The lookup service is Bolster's to choose (Google Places, Mapbox, Radar…); the system needs only the answer's shape.
  • Loading screenComponentTrying out

    One of sixteen drawings at random, a line from its trade under it, while an app first loads.

    • It lives in concepts/shared, not in system/; it moves once approved (D90).
    • No picture of the sixteen drawings outside the concepts: their CSS (about 390 KB) loads only there.
  • App shellComponentTrying out

    The dark chrome around every page: top bar, the menu, the page header, the phone's tab bar, the canvas grid.

    • The system's shell.css is the first design (rail and section sidebar, D24–D26); the one in use, one dark menu with accordions (D88–D98, trying out), lives in concepts/shared and hasn't moved into the system.
    • No breadcrumbs or back link for records deeper than a list (see Breadcrumbs).
  • Navigation at each widthPatternTrying out

    The menu pinned, folded or a drawer; the phone's bars that go as you scroll.

    • D88's title says the top bar is a footer on phones; its body and the shell have no top bar below 1024, only the floating menu button. D98 and I9 give the tablet menu 256 wide; the shell draws 272.
  • Global searchPatternTrying out

    One field in the top bar finds any record by type; it finds, it doesn't create.

    • Recents as words or records, what else should match, typo tolerance and a results page are undecided; a found record opens its list, not the record.
  • Forms and validationPatternTrying out

    Forms that fill themselves (D140): start from what's there, every value marked with where it came from, the record a page whose values change in place; underneath, a form done well (D134) for every field that remains: one column, labels above, errors in words, one Save.

    • C's rules are drafted, waiting for review (docs/forms-c-research.md, 06): records with labels beside values, values that belong together edited as one, documents in the trade's layout; marks naming the source, evidence one click away, no percentages; checks gated by the step, three severities, a summary before the step. Built into the forms concept's C views for review (concepts/forms/c.js); none is in the system yet.
    • C's parts are drafts in concepts/forms: a value changed in place, the source marks, the invoice as a document with its states. The model's four properties per field (neededBy, from, editable, usedBy) and a form recipe aren't started.
    • No side sheet for a form yet: the record panel (D30) and the dialog's sheet variant are close.
    • Still open: a value saved by Enter alone or by its Save; which sources first; whether a sent invoice or estimate is changed and resent or frozen behind “Reopen for changes”; labels on settings pages; which forms work offline.

Stable, with open items 27

Decided and safe to build on; these are the loose ends.

  • ColourFoundationStable

    Primitives and semantic roles: warm in light, cool in dark, every text pair AA.

    • The dark theme is contrast-checked but not yet designed in Figma (D14, proposed).
    • Status colours are the job's lifecycle; invoices, estimates, vendor orders and tasks borrow them case by case, with no written map from each thing's statuses to a colour.
    • No palette for charts: the summary cards' graphs reuse status colours, and a dashboard would need categorical and sequential scales.
    • Raspberry, teal and cyan have three or four steps and purple has no 950: fine while nothing semantic uses them, a gap the day something does.
    • In dark, tertiary text and the accent on the pitch-black 900 surfaces (a table's header, a pointed-at menu row, an open chip, a form's “(optional)” in a dialog) come to 4.3–4.4:1, just under AA; npm run contrast doesn't check those pairs.
  • TypographyFoundationStable

    Cera Pro for the interface, Space Grotesk for titles, eleven type roles on the 4px grid.

    • Cera Pro is licensed and not shipped with the system: anything outside the product (this site, the published concepts) falls back to Inter.
    • Long-form reading (a proposal's text, terms, emails) has no roles of its own; the eleven roles are for the interface.
    • Whisper, the script face the audit found in the product, has no place in the system yet: kept for signatures, or retired.
    • Components set their type from tokens, not the role classes, and some step outside the roles: a card's title is 18/28 (not the card-title role's 16/24), the record's amount is 30 with a line height off the 4px grid, a phone row's name has a 22px line.
    • Nothing uses the display role. D6 lists 11 roles and Space Grotesk for titles only; utilities.css has 12 and the amounts use Space Grotesk too.
  • Spacing and layoutFoundationStable

    The 4px grid, control heights, content widths and the four breakpoints.

    • Breakpoints are written in media queries and prose, not as tokens (CSS can't use a custom property in a media query): a custom-media or build step would make them one source.
    • No layout grid for dashboards and forms (columns, gutters, how cards span).
    • Density is settled per component (the pipeline's card size) but not as a system-wide choice.
    • The list page breaks at 1280 and 1440 (the record beside the list), which D15 doesn't list, and D15's 640 isn't used by any system CSS. D15 also gives the section sidebar as 300; the token is 256 (D26).
  • IconographyFoundationStable

    Font Awesome 6 Pro: Regular in the page, Light in the chrome, one meaning one icon.

    • 23 icon picks (for vendor orders, tasks and the pipeline's display settings) are proposed and waiting for review (D58); six of them are recorded only as proposals, so the vocabulary page doesn't show them.
    • Icon sizes are set per component; there's no size scale to pick from.
    • The icon set is licensed to Bolster: anything published outside Bolster's projects can't carry it.
  • AccessibilityFoundationStable

    WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor, checked on every page by machine, and what a machine can't check.

    • No recorded pass with a screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) on the built pages.
    • Text over a table's sticky column is counted by the checks but still needs a person's look.
    • Touch targets are 44 on coarse pointers for small buttons; nothing checks the rest.
    • A small button's touch area grows only in height (D5 says 44): an icon-only small button is 44 by 32 to a finger.
  • ButtonComponentStable

    Five roles with a body that lifts and sinks, four sizes, loading and icon-only.

    • Primary's hover is still open: invert (the prototype) or darken (the live app), D4.
    • No split button (a main action with a menu of its siblings: Save and Save as), which Bolster uses on records.
    • No button group for a run of related buttons that aren't a segmented choice.
  • MenuComponentStable

    Actions in a popover, nested menus that drill on phones, keys from the platform.

    • A checked item that also has a check icon draws two checks outside nested menus.
    • The arrow keys and aria-expanded for a plain menu live in the specimens' script, not the system; menu.css points to a specimen page that doesn't exist.
  • FieldComponentStable

    Text, text area, native select, money, numbers and search inputs, with label, helper and error; phones, emails and postal codes tidied by the company's country.

    • Phones outside the US and Canada are kept as written, not grouped by each country's own rules (that takes a library such as libphonenumber).
    • No hint for a likely typo in an email's domain (gmial.com).
    • Numbers are typed only (D137): − and + buttons wait for a case. The first candidate: the pipeline's Schedule dialog, Working days (1 to 60).
    • Do estimators type formulas (12*8) into quantities today? A quantity takes a number only.
  • Search fieldComponentStable

    Bare in a toolbar with a hover fill, ⌘K as a keycap; on the page, an icon that grows into its field.

    • D42's keycap was replaced by D112's separate ⌘ and K keycaps without D42 being marked.
    • Lists put what search looks in into the placeholder (D46); the concept's placeholder says “Search” and keeps the hint in the tooltip (D104).
  • Filter chipComponentStable

    One filter of a list: set ones solid with their value and a remove, Add filter for the rest.

    • Add filter's menu has a “Filter by” heading in the lists and the timeline but none in Tasks, and a chip's picker lines up differently in Tasks (D126 left them apart).
  • Checkbox, radio, toggleComponentStable

    Native inputs restyled, one “on” colour, keyboard and forms for free.

    • Two “on” colours, where D7 says one: the toggle uses control-on (yellow in dark), checkboxes and radios control-selected (near white in dark).
  • Calendar and date rangeComponentStable

    A month to click or arrow through, and the date range picker on it: presets, From and To, two months.

    • No time field: the calendar's new event dialog picks times in its own way (a draft).
    • No single-date picker: the to-do's due date builds one from the month with its own arrows, and its Period filter uses the browser's date inputs.
    • Which date the range filters by (created or last activity), team presets and comparisons are open (D37).
  • AvatarComponentStable

    People and companies: initials on one of eight AA colours, three sizes.

    • No photos are supplied: avatar.css fills the circle with an <img>, but every person shows initials.
    • No stack (several people on one job) beyond what the to-do's assignees draw.
    • The calendar and the pipeline each draw their own 20px avatar; the system has no size under 24.
    • avatar.css's header says companies are squares; D86 and D87 give them the round avatar, and there's no square variant.
  • Data tableComponentStable

    Rows by keyboard, a link over each row, sortable headers, numbers to the right, four-cornered rows on phones.

    • No editable cells: Bolster's sheets (items, the estimate) edit in cells and save together, which the table doesn't do (the bolster-page skill sends those away).
    • No tree rows in the system: the estimate concept's assemblies and items are its own (draft).
  • Summary cardsComponentStable

    A list's work queues or its KPIs: cards that filter, and one insight card.

    • I8 says filter cards are a radio group (one tab stop, arrows); summary-cards.css makes each a toggle button (aria-pressed), each its own tab stop.
  • TooltipComponentStable

    Names every icon without a label, on hover and focus, drawn from data-tip.

    • The CSS-only tooltip (.tooltip-anchor) is clipped by any card, table or sheet it sits in; only data-tip escapes. I4 says 150ms, tooltip.js waits 300ms.
  • ToastComponentStable

    One live region; errors stay, the rest leave after five seconds, Undo on the brand action.

    • The shell's toasts don't keep the contract (I5): they leave after 4s (5 with Undo), don't pause while pointed at or focused, and errors don't stay.
    • The toast region sits above a bottom tab bar (D29) that the menu being tried (D88) no longer has on phones.
  • PopoverComponentStable

    Panels under their trigger: filter options, display settings; a bottom sheet on phones.

    • Where a popover opens is worked out by the concepts' shell script (placePopover); nothing in system/ places it, and D16's anchor positioning isn't used.
  • Record panelComponentStable

    One row's details beside the list, or a side sheet: its next step first, then everything it links to.

    • Moving the second action into More when both don't fit, and folding a long activity into “+ n more events”, are done by the list concept's script, not the system.
  • TabsComponentStable

    Panels of one page, arrow-key navigation.

    • The arrow-key behaviour exists only in the specimens' script; the system ships none.
    • Sizes are a step off the control scale: the default draws 40 (medium elsewhere), md draws 48, and sm is offered but not styled.
  • ToolbarComponentStable

    The row over a list or a board: filter, search, dates, display, and the rule under it.

    • On the page, Add filter is an icon-only ghost (D104), copied into pipeline.css and todo.css instead of living in toolbar.css.
    • toolbar.css still carries .field-button for the estimate concept (D78), to remove when it moves to the secondary button.
  • Floating barComponentStable

    Bulk actions for ticked rows: a count, three actions then More; the tab bar's place on phones.

    • Which actions take the three slots and which go to More (D36) is worked out by the list concept's script, not the system.
  • Opening a recordPatternStable

    A row click peeks or opens; a row's next step leads its record, never a button on the row.

    • Whether vendor orders and expenses have a window of their own is open (D69); the concepts build no windows, opening one only says so in a toast.
  • Bulk actionsPatternStable

    Checkboxes only where there are bulk actions; the bar counts and acts, select mode on phones.

    • Shift-click to tick a range is in the contract (I6) but not built in the list concept.
  • Focus and keyboardPatternStable

    One focus ring, focus returned where it came from, busy buttons and live areas that keep their shape.

    • Two breaks of I13 remain in the list concept: the first chip pushes the table down, and filtering to nothing collapses the table under the pointer.
  • List pagePage typeStable

    Jobs, invoices, vendors, the catalog: cards, a table card, the record beside it; built from a model and an intent.

    • The chips row pushes the table down when the first filter is added (I13).
  • Tokens and CSSToolingStable

    Plain CSS in layers, importable as a package: tokens, base, components, utilities.

    • package.json's files leave out system/components/tooltip.js, so an install from GitHub misses it.
