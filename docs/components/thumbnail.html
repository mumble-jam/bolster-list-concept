Components

Thumbnail

A photo of a thing, such as a catalog item, in a rounded square. Where there's no photo, or it hasn't loaded, a placeholder holds its place.

Stablecomponents/thumbnail.cssUpdated 2026-10-15

When to use

What sits before a row's name is a slot (D55, D86). It holds the thing itself:

  • A thumbnail when the thing has pictures: the catalog's items. Rows without a photo show the placeholder, so the names still line up.
  • An avatar when the row is itself a person or company. People are circles; things are squares.
  • Nothing otherwise. A list doesn't take a slot because another list has one.

Anatomy

  1. Box. A rounded square on a quiet tint.
  2. Placeholder. The image icon, 40% of the box, in the tertiary text colour.
  3. Photo. Covers the placeholder once it has drawn, cropped to fill the square.
  4. Edge. A hairline inside the edge, only with a photo, so a white photo still reads as a square on a white row.

Sizes

  • Medium, 40, the default: the size in a row's slot.
  • Small, 24, with tighter corners (4 instead of 8), and large, 64, for places outside a row.
  • On phones a list row's thumbnail is 48, down the left of the whole item.

States

The photo lies on top of the placeholder, so the placeholder shows while the photo loads, where there's none, and when its link breaks: onerror="this.remove()" on the image takes it away. Without that, browsers draw their own broken-image mark over the square.

In a list and a record

  • In a row, the thumbnail leads the name and its detail line, like an avatar.
  • On phones a list with photos keeps them, a column down the left of each item; a list without any drops the slot.
  • The record shows the row's photo large at its top, the card's width at 3:2, with the credit its licence asks for as a caption. A broken link takes the whole figure away; a row without a photo shows none there (Record).

Usage

Accessibility

  • Beside a name the photo says nothing the name doesn't: alt="". The placeholder icon is decorative.
  • The thumbnail isn't a control; the row's link opens the thing.
