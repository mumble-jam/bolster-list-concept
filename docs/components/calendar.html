Calendar and date range

A month to click or arrow through, and the date range picker built on it: presets first, From and To that take typing, two months for the rest. It's how a list filters by its date.

Stablecomponents/calendar.cssUpdated 2026-10-19

When to use

  • Use the date range picker to filter a list by its main date: created, due, sent. A list with a date column offers it, in the toolbar beside the search (D46).
  • Use the calendar (one month) wherever a day is picked from a grid. In the system it's the picker's; the to-do list also uses it under a task's due date presets (a draft, To-do).
  • Not for a schedule. Jobs and visits laid out on days are the calendar page, a page type of its own (Calendar).

There's no time field yet: the calendar page's new event dialog picks times its own way.

The date button

The picker opens from a secondary button in the toolbar that reads back what's set. At rest it says “All time”. Once set, it names the date it filters by, then the preset or the dates, the month as a word so 9/10 can't be read two ways.

On phones only the value shows (the date's name goes), and the button gives way before the search field when the bar runs out of room.

Anatomy

  1. Presets. A rail of ten, short spans to long, All time first: All time, Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This quarter, Year to date, Last 12 months. Then Custom. The picked one is tinted and medium.
  2. From and To. Two small fields that take typing. Empty From reads “Any”, empty To “Today”.
  3. Two months side by side, the later one ending at this month, with arrows to page back and on. The forward arrow is off at this month for a date that can only be past.
  4. Foot. What it filters by, the span's length and the time zone (“By created date · 26 days · Eastern time”), then Cancel and Apply.

The month and its days

One month is a table: weekday letters on top, days as buttons in cells. Weeks start on Monday, the working week first and the weekend together at its end. It's one setting, the shell's, read by everything that draws a month or moves by week (D76).

Behaviour

  • Presets apply at once and close the picker.
  • A span built by hand waits for Apply. The first day picked starts it, the second ends it (an earlier day swaps them); while it's half built, the band follows the pointer or the focus. Cancel, Escape or a click outside leave the filter as it was.
  • Typing is always possible. From and To read “Sep 8, 2026”, “8 Sep 2026”, “Sep 8”, “9/8/2026” and “2026-09-08” on Enter or on leaving the field, and the months move to show it. A date that isn't one, or comes after today for a past date, is flagged in the foot and Apply waits.
  • A span that matches a preset is that preset, and reads as it.
  • Saved views and page descriptions hold a preset's id or a span whose ends can be days from today, so “More than 90 days ago” is a span with no start (“..-91”). Old links map across.

On phones

The same bottom sheet as every other menu. Six presets are the list (All time, Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month), then Custom…, which drills in to From and To, the last twelve months and this one in a column that scrolls, and Apply pinned to the bottom. See it on the list concept at phone width.

Usage

  • One date range per list, on its main date.
  • The ten presets are the system's, the same on every list. Team presets are still open.

Content

  • Dates read month first, the year once at the end: “Oct 7”, “Wed, Oct 14, 2026”, a span “Oct 12 – 18, 2026” or “Sep 28 – Oct 4, 2026” (D103).
  • The button: “All time”; “Created · Last 30 days”; “Created · Sep 8 – Oct 3, 2026”; a span with no start, “Created · Until Jul 9, 2026”.
  • The foot names the date, the length and the time zone: “By created date · 26 days · Eastern time”. Days are the company's days, and it says so.

Accessibility

  • The month is a table role="grid" labelled by its title, with the weekday's full name in each header's abbr.
  • Each day's button has its full date as its name (“Thursday, October 8, 2026, today”); the picked ends carry aria-selected="true".
  • The grid is one tab stop: the focus day has tabindex="0", the arrows move it.
  • The button says it opens a dialog (aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded) and its accessible name holds the value: “Created: Last 30 days. Change”.
  • The weekday letters and today's outline use the secondary ink: the tertiary one and the strong line were too faint on white. Every pair passes AA.

What's still open

  • No single-date picker: the to-do's due date builds one from the month with its own arrows, and its Period filter uses the browser's date inputs.
  • Which date the range filters by (created or last activity), team presets and comparisons are open (D37).
