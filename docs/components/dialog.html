Dialog

The platform's own dialog, in three variants: centred for confirmations and forms, a sheet for long pickers, and the workspace for a job. Focus goes back where it came from.

Stablecomponents/dialog.cssUpdated 2026-09-29

A dialog is a native <dialog> opened with showModal(). The browser does the hard parts: Escape closes it, focus moves inside and stays there, the page behind goes inert, and focus returns to whatever opened it. The system adds the look, three sizes and two more variants. Before, Bolster's overlays ignored Escape and dropped focus on the page (D16).

When to use

  • Use a dialog when the page has to stop for an answer: a confirmation before something that can't be undone, a short form (a new event, a step in the pipeline), a choice from a long list.
  • Use a popover for a quick choice tied to one control, such as a filter's options or a row's actions (Popover).
  • Use the record panel to look at one row's details without leaving the list (Record panel).
  • Use a toast to say something happened, with Undo where it can be undone (Toast).

Anatomy

  1. Header (.dialog-header). The title as an h2, 64px tall, a rule under it.
  2. Close (optional). A ghost icon button named “Close”. A confirmation leaves it out: its two buttons are the way out.
  3. Body (.dialog-body). 24px padding. The only part that scrolls, so the header and footer stay put.
  4. Footer (.dialog-footer). The buttons, at the end, the main action last. A secondary Cancel lies flat on the dialog (D78).

Variants

Centred

The default: in the middle of the screen over a scrim, for confirmations, forms and filters. It fades in and grows from 97% over 240ms, eased out, and reverses as it closes. It's never taller than 85% of the screen (52rem at most).

SizeWidthUse it for
sm400A confirmation: a question, a line of what follows, two buttons.
md (default)560A short form: a new event, a step in the pipeline (Create a job, Book a visit).
lg800Filters and columns together.

On a narrow screen a dialog is never wider than the screen less 16px a side.

Sheet: a picker

For choosing one thing from a long list, such as a company type. The trigger is a field-like button showing the current value; the sheet has a search field, the options as radios, and Done. “Add a new type” sits in the footer, never above the options. On desktop it's centred; below 768 it comes up from the bottom, full width, with a handle and 16px top corners, at most 80% of the screen tall (I2). It's the one variant a click on the scrim closes (closedby="any"), since nothing is lost.

Workspace: the job

The job's own window, where people work on it: the screen less 16px all round, with 16px corners, a dark header carrying the client, the job, its status and its next step, and the job's own navigation down the left. On phones it fills the screen. It's what a row opens when the thing has a window of its own (Opening records). While it's open the address carries ?job=, so back, forward and shared links work; Escape closes it unless a cell in the estimate is being edited, which Escape cancels first (I7).

Its close button takes the chrome's inks by hand for now, as above: the dialog's stylesheet doesn't yet style buttons on its dark header.

Behaviour

What happens
OpeningA button calls showModal(). The scrim fades in, the dialog fades and grows into place.
FocusMoves to the first thing that takes focus, or the one marked autofocus. In a confirmation that's the safe choice (“Keep job”). Tab cycles inside; the page behind can't be reached.
ClosingEscape, Close, or the footer's cancel. A click on the scrim only where nothing can be lost (the sheet).
AfterFocus goes back to what opened it. If that's gone (the row was archived), to the list's heading; never to the page's body (I11).

Usage

  • Keep the scrim click off wherever typing could be lost.
  • The filled red button is only ever a confirmation's final button.

Content

  • Title: a question for a confirmation (“Archive this job?”), a name for anything else (“Columns and filters”, “Company type”). Sentence case, no full stop.
  • Body of a confirmation: one line on what follows and how to get it back, if you can.
  • Buttons: a verb and its object (“Archive job”, “Keep job”), not OK, Yes or Confirm.

Accessibility

  • <dialog> is a modal dialog to assistive technology. Name it with aria-labelledby pointing at its heading; a confirmation also takes aria-describedby pointing at its line.
  • The trigger says it opens a dialog: aria-haspopup="dialog". A picker's trigger shows the chosen value once the sheet closes.
  • Every close button has an accessible name: “Close”, or “Close job” in the workspace.
  • In the picker, typing filters the list, arrow keys move between options, Enter or Done confirms, and Escape closes without changing the value (I2).
  • With reduced motion the dialog and its scrim appear without moving (base.css takes every duration away).
