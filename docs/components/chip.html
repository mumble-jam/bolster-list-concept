Filter chip

One filter of a list, once it's set: the field, its value and a remove, solid in the toolbar's second row. Add filter adds the rest; Clear all takes them all away.

Stablecomponents/chip.csscomponents/toolbar.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-11-05

When to use

  • Use a chip to show a filter that's narrowing a list, board or timeline, and to change or remove it there.
  • Use Add filter to add one. The toolbar shows Add filter and the filters that are set, never empty ones waiting to be filled (D45).
  • Use the date range button for the list's main date: it reads back its value in the toolbar's first row instead of becoming a chip (Calendar and date range).
  • Use a badge for a record's status or category. A chip is always a control (Badge).
  • Use a segmented control to switch a view or a mode; a chip narrows what's in it (Segmented control).

Anatomy

  1. Body. 32px tall, a solid hairline, a 12px corner and the header's fill. Its two buttons sit inside the edge, their corners a pixel tighter (D35).
  2. Field. The filter's name and a colon, in secondary ink.
  3. Value. What it's set to, medium weight, cut with an ellipsis past 14rem. The field and the value are one button: it reopens the filter's picker.
  4. Remove. A separate button, the ×, that takes the filter away.

States

Every state, held in place, is in the live specimen at the end of this page.

  • Hover is colour, not a fill: the two buttons sit too close for two backgrounds to look right. Over the field and value, both turn link blue together; over the ×, it goes darker.
  • Picker open: the chip reads as hovered, and its edge turns the accent blue (D21).
  • Added, not set: in the lists only, a filter picked from Add filter whose picker was closed with nothing chosen. A dashed pill with a plus, that opens its picker.

Behaviour

The chips' behaviour is the shell's, written once and used by the lists, Tasks, the pipeline and the timeline (BolsterShell.filterChips, D126). A page says what removing and clearing do; the rest is shared.

  • The second row. The chips sit on a row under the toolbar, after the count (“12 of 133 jobs”). It shows once something narrows the list.
  • Changing a filter. The chip opens the filter's picker under it. The picker stays on its chip while the chips are drawn again as values change; if its last value is turned off and the chip goes, the picker stays where it is.
  • Removing one. The × takes the filter off, and focus goes to Add filter (or where the page names: the timeline's next chip).
  • Clear all follows the chips whenever one is set, as a small ghost button. It takes every filter off and focus goes to Add filter.
  • Add filter lists what's left to add, the pinned filters first (at most three, usually status, owner and a date, D45). It's disabled once nothing is left.
  • On phones the count and the chips stay on one line that scrolls sideways, its edge fading while there's more, so a chip never takes two lines and Clear all never one of its own (D103).

Open: Add filter's menu has a “Filter by” heading in the lists and the timeline but not in Tasks, and a chip's picker lines up with the chip's outer edge in Tasks, 1px in elsewhere.

Usage

Content

  • Field: the filter's name as the column says it, with a colon: “Status:”, “Owner:”.
  • Value: one or two values in full (“Pending, Booked”); three or more as a count (“3 selected”). A range reads “$5,000–$20,000”, “$5,000 or more” or “Up to $20,000”. A date reads as the date button does (“Last 30 days”).
  • A summary card's filter reads as the card, not the values it stands for: “Missing documents”.
  • A search in the lists is a chip too: “Search:” and the words in quotes.

Accessibility

  • Two real buttons, each a tab stop. The main one says what it is and what it does: aria-label="Status: Pending, Booked. Change filter", with aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded while its picker is open.
  • The × is named by what it removes: aria-label="Remove status filter", with a tooltip.
  • Focus never falls to the page when a chip goes: it moves to Add filter, or where the page names.
  • The count before the chips is a live region, so a screen reader hears the new total as filters change.
