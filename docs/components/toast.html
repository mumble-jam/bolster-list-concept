Components

Toast

Short feedback on an action someone just took, in one live region at the page's corner. Success and info leave on their own; errors stay until dismissed.

Stablecomponents/toast.cssUpdated 2026-10-25

When to use

  • Use a toast to confirm or report the result of an action someone just took that has no other visible result where they are: a proposal sent, a job archived, a vendor order cancelled from the bulk bar.
  • Not for a field's error. It goes on the field, where it can be fixed (Field).
  • Not for a list that didn't load. That's the banner in the list itself (Empty, loading and error).
  • Not for anything people need to keep, or that didn't come from their action. A toast leaves.

Anatomy

  1. Tone edge. A 4px edge in the tone's colour, on the inverse surface: dark in light, light in dark.
  2. Message. What happened, 14px, wrapping as it needs.
  3. Action (optional). One, usually Undo, in the brand colour made for the inverse surface (D125).
  4. Dismiss. An × that closes it now, with a tooltip.

Tones

Without a tone the edge takes the toast's own text colour.

Behaviour

WhenThe result of an action the person just took
WhereBottom right, up to 24rem wide, stacking upwards, the newest on top. On phones full width, above the bottom bar. Over an open modal dialog or sheet, the toasts move into it, so it doesn't cover them
ArrivesRises 8px as it fades in, 240ms
StaysSuccess and info leave after 5 seconds, paused while pointed at or focused. Errors stay until dismissed
LeavesAs it came, fading and sliding 8px, while its place closes up, so the toasts above come down rather than drop. Under reduced motion it only fades
FocusNever moves. The action and dismiss are in the tab order where they are

Content

Accessibility

  • One live region per page, aria-live="polite", present from the start: a toast added to it is read without moving focus.
  • Success, info and warning toasts are role="status"; errors are role="alert" and are read at once.
  • An error stays until dismissed, so there's time to read it; the others pause while pointed at or focused.
  • The × is named (“Dismiss”) and has a tooltip with the same word (D52).
  • Undo passes AA on the inverse surface in both themes, at rest and pointed at (D125).

What's still open

  • The toast region sits above a bottom tab bar (D29) that the menu being tried (D88) no longer has on phones.
