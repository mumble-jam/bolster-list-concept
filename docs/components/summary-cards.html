Components

Summary cards

A row of cards above a list: its KPIs, or every status of its lifecycle. A card is a button that filters the list; at most one insight card adds a figure people act on.

Stablecomponents/summary-cards.cssUpdated 2026-10-22

When to use

A list page may open with a summary, one of two kinds (D67). Nothing else goes above a list: no tabs, and no money bar (D68).

  • KPIs, the default: up to four figures that say how the list is doing and what needs someone. Invoices: Overdue, Not due yet, Paid · last 30 days, Draft.
  • The lifecycle, for pages where people move rows through their statuses (jobs, vendor orders): All, then every status in order, endings counted for the last 30 days. Rare endings stay in the Status filter.
  • A filter the page opens with (Active vendors) is the default view, never a card; All is a tap away.
  • Segments people come back to are saved views, not cards.

Anatomy

  1. Mark or dot. The status's mark on the job list (D49), its dot on other lists; none on All.
  2. Label. The status or slice, named as everywhere else on the screen.
  3. Count. How many rows, quiet, top right.
  4. Figure. Money where the list's key figure is money (D66), in Space Grotesk at 20px.

Status cards

  • A card is a work queue (D66): a status a task acts on, or a slice people work through (Missing documents). Four at most after All.
  • It's a button that applies one filter. It stays pressed in while it does (aria-pressed="true"), one at a time; pressing it again, or All, clears it. Its filter chip reads as the card: “Missing documents”, not “3 selected”.
  • Cards may overlap: an expense can be unpaid and submitted by its vendor.
  • An empty card keeps its place and greys its figure (data-empty), so the row doesn't change shape as filters change (I13).

The insight card

Information, not a filter: a rate, the counts behind it, its parts as a split bar, then one more figure. At most one per row, and only with a figure people act on.

  • The (?) opens a note that explains the figure in the screen's own words (D27): the same card, status and column names, in the same case. A term the screen doesn't show (won, decided) is defined once in the screen's labels, “Won is Booked plus In progress”, then used the same way everywhere.
  • When there's nothing to show, the bar becomes an empty track and the figure goes grey; nothing disappears.

Behaviour

  • The body is the cards' (Card). Status cards, being buttons, lift 1px when pointed at, sink at once when pressed, and a pressed one stays in, like a toggle that's on.
  • Width: status cards take equal columns and the insight card twice one (set --_slices to the number of status cards). Too narrow, and the row scrolls sideways rather than wrapping.
  • Phones: the insight card leads, so the overview is on the first screen, and the row scrolls sideways.

Graphs, trying out

Trying out (D116), on a second job list in the list concept: Job list with graphs. One of the two goes once it's decided.

Each status card draws its money over the last eight months as a small, ambient graph: a 1.5px line in the status's colour (All in neutral) over a wash of it, behind the card's lower 70%. It shows only while the card is pointed at or focused, never on touch screens, and never in the insight card. Each graph is scaled to its own range, so it shows a trend, not a size to compare. For now they're decorative and hidden from screen readers: no axis, values or readout.

Usage

Accessibility

  • The row is a role="group" named for what it does (“Filter by status”). Each status card is a <button> with aria-pressed; Tab moves between them.
  • The mark or dot is decoration beside the label; the label carries the status.
  • The insight card is a group named by its label; its split bar is an image whose aria-label says the parts in words.
  • The info button is named (“How win rate is worked out”) and has a tooltip with the same words (D52).
