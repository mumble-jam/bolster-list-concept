Empty, loading and error

What a part shows besides its content: skeletons in its shape while it loads, never a flash, a state when there's nothing yet or nothing matches, and a banner when it failed. Each sits where the content will be, so the page keeps its shape.

Needs workHigh prioritycomponents/states.csscomponents/loading.jsUpdated 2026-10-29

When to use

Every list designs four states besides its rows, and each has its own words and actions:

StateWhenShows
LoadingThe data hasn't arrived, for longer than 300 msSkeletons in the shape of what's coming: rows in the table, the summary cards, the record's details; the count says “Loading…”
EmptyNothing has been made here yetA state with what will show up and the actions that make the first one
No resultsFilters or search exclude everythingA state that names what's excluding everything and offers to undo it
ErrorThe list didn't loadA banner at the top of the table card, with Try again

For the result of an action someone took, use a toast; for a field that can't be saved, the error on the field (Field).

Live areas keep their shape

A list changes its values, never its layout, as people filter, search, sort, select and page (I13). Every state fills the same slots at the same size: the table card stays where it is, the summary keeps its cards, a missing figure becomes a word and a bar an empty track. A state is said by what a slot holds, never by adding or taking away lines.

Still open in the list concept: the chips row pushes the table down when the first filter is added, and filtering to nothing collapses the table under the pointer.

Loading

Skeletons stand where the content will be, in its shape and size: rows cell by cell in the columns' widths and alignment (numbers to the right), a card's label and figure, a record's details. What's already known shows at once: a page's title, toolbar and Create; a record's name and status from the row that was clicked. Nothing moves when the data arrives (D142).

The rules

  • No flash for fast loads. A skeleton waits 300 ms before it shows; once shown, it stays at least 300 ms, so a quick load doesn't flicker.
  • Same footprint. The skeleton takes the space the content will take; the layout never jumps.
  • No spinners for content. A spinner, or “Saving…”, is for an action someone took, as a busy Save.
  • Slow or failed. After 10 seconds the skeleton says so in words, “Still loading jobs…”. A failure puts the error state in its place, with Try again.
  • One announcement. The region loading is aria-busy="true"; screen readers hear “Loading jobs” once, never a bar at a time.
  • Still under reduced motion. The bars stop shimmering; they stay.
  • The loading screen is only for the app's first open (Loading screen, D90). A page opened after that gets its skeleton, never the loading screen.

Where it applies

PartShows at onceStands in
A list's rowsThe table's headerAbout eight rows of bars, the select box as a square
The summary cardsEach card, at its sizeA bar for the label, a taller one for the figure (data-shape="figure")
The recordThe name and status, from the rowA few rows of label and value bars, not an exact copy
More rowsThe rows already thereFour rows of bars at the end; on a failure, the error and Try again in the list's foot
A page's first loadThe title, the toolbar, Create, the filter buttonsThe cards' and the table's skeletons; the count says “Loading…”

Every one of them, live, with the timing to try, is in the specimen at the end of this page.

Empty

Nothing has been made here yet. Say what shows up here and how it gets here, using the list's noun (D28), and offer the action that makes the first one. The toolbar hides: there's nothing to search or filter.

No results

Filters or a search exclude everything. Name what's excluding it, in the screen's own words (D27): the chips' fields and values and the search, as they read on screen. Offer to undo each: Clear all filters when filters are set, Clear search when there's a search.

Error

The list didn't load. A banner at the top of the table card says what didn't load and what to do, with Try again; the summary cards hide until it does. In a card, the banner sits 8px in from the card's edges, so its corners share the card's (D35).

Usage

Accessibility

  • The error banner is role="alert", so it's read as it appears.
  • A loading part is aria-busy="true"; skeletons have no text and say nothing; “Loading jobs” is announced once, politely, and “Still loading jobs…” after 10 seconds is a status.
  • A state's title is a heading, so it's found by its words; its actions are real buttons.
  • The shimmer stops under reduced motion; the skeletons stay.
  • The banner's text and icon pass AA on its tint in light and dark.

What's still open

  • The banner exists for errors only: there's no inline alert for information, a warning or a success that stays on the page (see Inline alert).
  • A first-use empty state (nothing has ever been made here) reads the same as “no results”; they need different words and actions.
