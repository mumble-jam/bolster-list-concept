Segmented control

Two to four options of which one is always picked: a view or a mode, switched in place. Native radios with a button's corners, so it sits level with the buttons beside it.

Stablecomponents/segmented-control.cssUpdated 2026-10-20

When to use

  • Use a segmented control to change how the same thing is shown or worked on, in place: the calendar's Month, Week, Day and Crew; an estimate's Profit or Markup; a theme's Auto, Light and Dark.
  • Use tabs to switch between panels of one page that hold different things: a job's Overview, Estimate and Takeoff (Tabs).
  • Use filter chips or summary cards to narrow a list. A segmented control never hides records (Filter chip, Summary cards).
  • Use radios for a choice in a form that's saved with it, or for options that need more than a word (Checkbox, radio, toggle).
  • Use a select or a menu past four options.

Anatomy

  1. Track. A sunken fill with an 8px corner, the same as a button's (D77), and 2px of room around the options.
  2. Picked option. Raised out of the track on the raised surface with a small shadow. Its corner is concentric with the track's: 8 less the 2px inset, 6 (D35).
  3. Option. A label around a hidden native radio. Medium weight in both states, so nothing shifts when it's picked.

Sizes

On the control scale, so it lines up with buttons and fields in the same row (D5). Small is the default, for toolbars.

States

Hover and focus are held in place. Hover darkens an option's words; focus is the ring around the option, as a keyboard moves through the group.

Every state, held in place, is in the live specimen at the end of this page.

Usage

Content

  • One or two words per option, sentence case, the same kind of word across the group: “Month”, “Week”, “Day”.
  • The group is named for screen readers by what it switches: aria-label="View", “Margin type”.
  • An icon may lead a label, at 16px.

Accessibility

  • A native radio group (D7): one tab stop, and the arrow keys change the choice at once (I8). It works without script and sends its value with a form.
  • role="radiogroup" with an aria-label on the track.
  • The radio fills its option, invisible, so a click anywhere on the option picks it.
  • Labels use the secondary text colour, not the controls' off colour, which is made for edges (3:1) and fails as text.
