Loading screen

While the app first loads, one of sixteen drawings from the trades, picked at random, with a line from its trade under it. Trying out in the concepts.

Trying outconcepts/shared/loader.jsconcepts/shared/loaders.cssconcepts/shared/tape.jsUpdated 2026-10-21

Trying out (D90). It lives in concepts/shared, not in system/, and moves there once approved. See it in any concept: it plays on the first page you open in a session, and again from the concept controls (Show the loader, A slow load).

When it shows

  • The first page of a session, over everything, while the app loads. Once per session, not on every page.
  • Not for a list or a panel that's loading inside a page you're already on: those show skeletons in their own shape (Empty, loading and error).
  • Not for an action: a button that's working shows it itself (Button, busy).

What shows

The page's canvas, and centred on it one drawing above one line of text.

  • A drawing at random, one of sixteen line drawings: a tape measure, a nut running down a bolt, a wrench bolting the Bolster mark together, a little brick house, dividers walking a line, a row of nails, a hammer, screws, three paint rollers, a drill, a taping knife and a spirit level. Never the same one twice running.
  • A line from its trade under it, naming what that tool is doing, with fading dots after it. About one in five is in the trade's own words. After the first, one in three comes from the site or the office instead. A new line every 2 seconds or so.
  • Every drawing keeps the same band, so the line sits in the same place whatever is drawn. A tape measure and its line share a width: the blade, run out, ends where the line ends.
UnderLines like
The tape measureMeasuring twice · Reading the sixteenths · Allowing for the kerf
The taping knifeBedding the tape · Feathering the edges · Sanding to a level 4 finish
The spirit levelChasing the bubble · Shimming it plumb · Checking it's dead level
The dividersLaying out the job · Marking the studs · Sixteen on centre
Any drawingWaiting on the lumber yard · Going over the change orders · Counting sheets of drywall

How it ends

DoneThe drawing finishes (a tape snaps shut, a scene settles), its done line shows for a beat (“Nailed it”), then the screen fades away in 260ms
SlowAfter 10 seconds it says so plainly, “This is taking longer than usual. Still opening Job list”, and offers Retry, which starts a new load
In the conceptsThe wait is a stand-in of about 4 seconds

Content

Lines are dry and specific, about the work: what the tool in the drawing is doing. No puns, and never a promise of speed.

The page's name isn't in the line: it goes to screen readers. On screen, only the slow message names it.

Motion

  • A new line rises 3px as it fades in, held at the width it ends at so a centred line doesn't sway. Five other ways are being tried in the concept controls: roll, typed, decoded, letter by letter and into focus.
  • Reduced motion: the drawing stands still and the lines change without moving.

Accessibility

  • A status says “Opening Job list” once; the drawing and the lines are hidden from screen readers.
  • The page under it is aria-busy="true" until the screen has gone.
  • When it's slow, the status says the slow message, and Retry is a real button.

Code

The app shell owns it: BolsterShell.loading() puts the cover up and plays a load; BolsterShell.loading({ slow: true }) plays the slow one. The cover (in shell.css) goes up at once, so the page never flashes; the drawings' animations are about 390 KB of CSS, so loaders.css, tape.js and loader.js load only when the screen plays.

BolsterShell.loading();               // a load, ending in the drawing's finish
BolsterShell.loading({ slow: true }); // keeps going; after 10 s the slow line and Retry

loaders.css and tape.js are generated from the drawings' source by scripts/build-loaders.mjs; change the source and run it again rather than editing them.

What's still open
