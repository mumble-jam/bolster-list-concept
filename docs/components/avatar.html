BolsterDesign system

Components

Avatar

A person or a company as their initials in a circle, on one of eight colours that all pass AA with white. It appears only where the thing is a person or company.

Stablecomponents/avatar.cssUpdated 2026-09-29

When to use

An avatar shows a person or a company, and only where the thing on screen is one (D86, D87).

  • A row that is a person or company: customers, subs and vendors lead their name with an avatar.
  • A link to a person or company: a Vendor or Client column, or the vendor and the client in a record's related links.
  • Teammates: the people in an Owner or Created by column, and a to-do's assignees.
  • Not a row named after someone. A job named after its client, an invoice after its customer or a vendor order after its vendor takes no avatar: the name already says who, and the slot would cost every row its width.
  • Things with pictures (a catalog item) take a thumbnail: people are circles, things are squares.

Colours

Eight backgrounds, each above 4.5:1 with the white initials, in light and dark (D13). The live product generated its colours, and some failed, a bright green among them.

Pick data-color from a stable hash of the person's or company's id, never at random, so someone keeps their colour on every page and every visit. Colour only helps tell people apart; it means nothing.

Sizes

  • Small, 24, beside a name in a table cell (Owner, Created by) and in menus.
  • Medium, 32, the default: in a row's slot before its name.
  • Large, 40, the height of a medium control, to line up beside one.

In a table

In a row's slot, the avatar sits before the name and its detail line. In a person cell it sits before the name, small. On phones a row drops its avatar, and the name takes the width.

Usage

Content

  • Two initials. They show in capitals whatever is typed.
  • An <img> inside fills the circle, but nothing supplies photos yet: people show their initials.

Accessibility

  • Beside a name, the avatar repeats it: aria-hidden="true", so a screen reader hears the name once.
  • Standing alone (a to-do's assignee), it's named: role="img" and aria-label with the full name, and a tooltip with the same words (data-tip, D52). Initials don't count as a label.
  • Every colour passes AA with its initials (npm run contrast).

Every variant and state

Its specimen, live: the same markup and CSS a page uses, working (point, press, type), in this page's theme.

What's still open

  • No photos are supplied: avatar.css fills the circle with an <img>, but every person shows initials.
  • No stack (several people on one job) beyond what the to-do's assignees draw.
  • The calendar and the pipeline each draw their own 20px avatar; the system has no size under 24.
  • avatar.css's header says companies are squares; D86 and D87 give them the round avatar, and there's no square variant.

All of it in the backlog
