Popover

A small panel that opens under the control that asked for it: a filter's options, display settings, a row's actions. On phones every popover is a bottom sheet.

Stablecomponents/popover.cssUpdated 2026-11-02

A popover is the list page's panel: it holds a filter's options, the display settings, a row's ··· actions, Add filter's menu. It's a native popover, so Escape and a click outside close it and it sits in the top layer, over every card and bar. It opens under its trigger and stays on it as the page or a board scrolls. On phones the same markup becomes a bottom sheet.

When to use

  • Use a popover for a short choice made in place, tied to one control: narrowing a list by a field, picking a setting, acting on one row.
  • Use a menu for a plain list of actions on a button outside a list, such as the company and avatar menus (Menu). A popover's rows are the menu's rows, so the two look alike.
  • Use a dialog when the choice needs room, a form, or a decision that can't be undone (Dialog). A long picker (a company type) is a dialog's sheet, not a popover.
  • Use a tooltip to name an icon. A popover is never opened by hover alone (Tooltip).

Anatomy

  1. Panel (.pop). White in light, with the cards' edge, a shadow tinted with the page and 12px corners. 18rem wide, up to 26rem or 70% of the screen tall, then it scrolls inside itself.
  2. Head (.pop-head, optional). The filter's or setting's name, and Clear once something is set.
  3. Find (.pop-find, optional). A search box over a long list of options: any filter over records, or over more than twelve values (D44).
  4. Body (.pop-body). The rows: options (.option, a checkbox or radio with its label) or menu rows (.menu-item). Rows sit 8 in from the edge with 4px corners, so their curve follows the panel's 12 (D35).
  5. Count (.option-count, optional). How many rows each option would leave, given the other filters.

Kinds

Options

A filter's values, each a .option: checkboxes where several can be picked (Status, Owner), radios where one can (a period, with Any time first). A pick applies at once; there's no Apply button.

A range

A number filter is two fields, Minimum and Maximum, in the type's unit ($ for money, % for a percentage, a suffix such as days), with Apply in a foot (.range, .pop-foot): what's typed waits for Apply rather than filtering at every keystroke.

Settings, with nested menus

Display settings is a list of settings. Each row names the setting, reads back its current value and opens a menu of its own beside it (.submenu-row, .submenu-trigger), with a check by the choice. On phones there's no room beside, so the row drills in: its items replace the sheet's rows under a header with Back and the menu's name (I15). The behaviour is defined once for every page; see Nested menus.

A row's actions

The ··· at a row's end opens its actions as menu rows: Open first, then the row's next step and the rest, a section heading over a group (.pop-section), a rule (hr) before Delete, which is red and last.

An item keeps the icon column (.menu-icon) only when items beside it have icons, so their labels line up, as Booked and In progress do under Duplicate. A menu with no icons has no empty column. Icons are 14px Regular in an 18px slot, in the secondary ink; a danger row's icon takes its red.

Placement

  • Under its trigger, 4px below, its right edge on the trigger's right, or its left edge on the trigger's left for a chip's picker. It moves above when there's no room below, and keeps 12px from the window's edges.
  • It stays on what opened it as the page or a board scrolls, and keeps its alignment through a resize or a redraw (D102).
  • A nested menu opens beside its row, on the side with room, its first item level with the row; below the row when neither side has room (I15).
  • Where a popover opens is worked out once, for every page, by the app shell's script (BolsterShell.placePopover in concepts/shared/shell.js). The stylesheet only draws it.

On phones

Below 768 every popover is a bottom sheet: full width from the bottom, 16px top corners, a handle, the scrim behind it. It slides up over 240ms. Its rows grow to a 44px touch target, and the sheet is at most 80% of the screen tall. Display settings that only matter on phones (Sort by, when the column headers are hidden) carry .pop-phone-only and disappear from 768 up.

Usage

  • Keep a popover to one task. A form, or a decision that needs explaining, is a dialog's.
  • A trigger's tooltip hides while its popover is open, so the two never overlap.

Content

  • Head: the field's name as the list's column says it (“Status”, “Owner”), not “Filter by status”.
  • A setting reads back its value (“Row height · Comfortable”), so the menu can be read without opening its rows.
  • Menu rows are verbs, and Open is named after what it opens: “Open job”, “Open invoice”.
  • The find box says what it finds: “Find owner”, “Find a job or client”.

Accessibility

  • The trigger says what it opens and whether it's open: aria-haspopup="menu" or "dialog", and aria-expanded.
  • A panel of actions is role="menu" with menuitem and menuitemradio rows (aria-checked on the chosen one); a panel of options is role="dialog" with an aria-label, its options a labelled group of real checkboxes or radios.
  • Arrow keys move between rows, skipping disabled and hidden ones; Home and End jump; Escape closes and focus goes back to the trigger (I3, I15).
  • Every row is at least 32px tall, 44px on touch screens.

Code

Class or attributeWhat it is
.pop[popover]The panel. role="menu" or role="dialog", with an aria-label.
.pop-headA heading (h3), and Clear where something is set.
.pop-findA find box over the options, in line with the rows.
.pop-bodyThe rows: .option or .menu-item, with .pop-section headings and hr rules.
.optionA label around a checkbox or radio, .option-label and .option-count.
.range, .pop-footMinimum and maximum fields, and the Apply foot.
.submenu-rowA row that opens a nested menu: .submenu-trigger holding .submenu-label and .submenu-value, then the menu, .pop.submenu.
.menu-iconAn 18px slot for a row's leading icon, empty to keep labels in line.
.pop-phone-onlyA row shown only below 768.
data-drill, data-drilledSet by the script on phones: the sheet has drilled into the marked nested menu.
