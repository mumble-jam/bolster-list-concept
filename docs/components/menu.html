Menu

A short list of actions or one-of settings behind a button. A native popover: Escape, a click outside and the top layer come from the platform. A row can open a menu of its own, beside it on a desktop and drilled into on a phone.

Stablecomponents/menu.csscomponents/popover.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-10-26

When to use

  • Use a menu for actions of equal weight that don't all need to show: a row's actions, the company and avatar menus, Download's formats. One button opens them (Button).
  • Use a menu for settings with a few fixed values, each row reading its value and opening its choices: Display settings' Group by, Row height and Columns.
  • Use a popover for a small panel with controls in it, like a filter's options or the date range picker (Popover).
  • Use a select for a value in a form (Field), and a segmented control for two to four views that should stay in sight (Segmented control).
  • Don't hide the main action in a menu. The one thing most people do here is a button of its own.

Anatomy

  1. Container. The raised surface, the cards' edge and a shadow tinted with the page. A 12px corner with its rows 8px in and 4px round, so the curves share a centre (D35). At least 12rem wide, at most 20rem.
  2. Icon (optional). 14px, in an 18px slot so the labels line up whatever the icon's shape. Kept on every row once one has it, empty where a row has none; left out of a menu where no row has one.
  3. Item. 32px tall (44 on touch), its label in sentence case. Hover fills it a step stronger than a row on the page.
  4. Section heading (optional). Names a group of rows, like a set of choices: “Change status”.
  5. Checked item. A one-of choice that's picked shows a check at the row's end.
  6. Destructive item. Red, last, after a separator.

Items

  • Action (menuitem): does something and closes the menu.
  • Choice (menuitemradio with aria-checked): picks one of a set; the picked one has the check.
  • Disabled: muted and skipped by the arrow keys. Prefer leaving out what can't apply here.
  • Danger (data-tone="danger"): red text, its icon red too.

Nested menus

A row with choices of its own reads its value and opens a second menu: a setting in Display settings, Change status, Download. Its name, the value in secondary ink, a chevron. The nested menu is as wide as its longest option, so the check sits close to the chosen one (I15).

  • On a desktop it opens beside the menu, on the side with room, its first item level with its row; below the row when there's no room either side. A click opens it, and so does a mouse resting on the row (120ms); resting on a row without one closes it (200ms).
  • On a phone every menu is a bottom sheet with no room beside it, so the row drills in: its items replace the sheet's rows, the sheet easing to its new height, under a header with Back on the left, the menu's name in the middle and a rule below. The whole header taps back.
  • A pick acts and closes the sheet; a tap outside or Escape closes it from any level.
  • The behaviour is the shell's, written once (BolsterShell.nestedMenus, D126): a page says what's in each menu and what a pick does.

Behaviour

OpeningA button with popovertarget and aria-haspopup="menu". The menu fades in and drops 4px, 120ms, easing out.
PositionBelow the button, lined up with its start edge, 4px under it; it flips above or across when there's no room (CSS anchor positioning). A browser without anchor positioning centres it.
FocusThe first item on opening; back to the button on closing.
Keys↑ ↓ move, Home and End jump, skipping disabled and hidden items; Enter or Space activates; Escape closes. In a nested menu, → Enter or Space on its row go in, ← or Escape come back out.
ClosingEscape, a click outside, or choosing an item.
PhonesMenus placed by the shell (.pop) become a bottom sheet with a handle, sliding up over a scrim; rows grow to 44px.

Usage

Content

  • Actions are verbs in sentence case: “Duplicate”, “Send reminder”, “Download PDF”.
  • Settings read as a name and the current value: “Row height”, “Comfortable”.

Accessibility

  • role="menu" with menuitem or menuitemradio rows, and an aria-label naming the menu.
  • The button says what it opens: aria-haspopup="menu", and aria-expanded that follows the menu. The audited company and avatar menus had the role but never said they were open.
  • A nested menu's row has aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at its menu.
  • An icon-only button that opens a menu has an aria-label and a tooltip with the same words (D52).
  • Focus is drawn inside the row (an inset ring), so it's never clipped by the menu's edge.
