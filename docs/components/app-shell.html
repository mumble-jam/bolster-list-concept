BolsterDesign system

Components

App shell

The chrome around every page: the top bar, the navigation, the page header and the canvas behind it. The system holds a first design; a second, one dark menu, is being tried out in the concepts.

Trying outcomponents/shell.cssconcepts/shared/shell.cssconcepts/shared/shell.jsUpdated 2026-11-05

The app shell is everything around a page's content: the dark top bar, the navigation, the page header and the faint grid behind the page. A page doesn't draw any of it. It asks the shell for it and passes only what's its own, such as which page is current and where its content starts. The chrome is dark in both themes, as Bolster's is today.

Two designs. system/components/shell.css holds the first design, measured on Bolster's live app: an icon rail, a light section sidebar beside it, and a bottom tab bar on phones. The concepts are trying out a second: one dark menu with every section in it, Settings as a window, and a floating menu button in place of both bars below 1024 (concepts/shared/shell.css and shell.js, on top of the system's file). The screenshots above are the second. Both share the top bar, the page header and the canvas grid. If the second stays, the system's shell and its specimen follow.

What every page gets

Top bar

60 tall, on the chrome's near-black. From the left: the logo in a cell as wide as the rail, the company switcher, the search in the middle (up to 672 wide), then Ask Bolton and your account as 36px circles (D24). The search is a real field: ⌘K, Ctrl+K or / from anywhere focuses it; it shows ⌘ K at rest, esc while focused and Clear once it holds words; its caret and selection are the logo's yellow (D112, trying out).

Page header

The page's name (h1) and its own actions at the end, the main one last (Export, then Create job). It's 64 tall, the same as the section sidebar's header, so the two rules under them meet in one line. On phones the name takes its own line and the actions wrap under it.

Page width

The page area (.app-content) centres its content up to a width chosen by what the page is for, with 24px padding (16 on phones).

(default)1280Anything without a reason to be wider.

The canvas grid

A faint grid lies behind every page, lit around the pointer in a soft ellipse that trails it a little; a quarter of it shows everywhere, so phones without a pointer still see it. In dark a soft blue-violet light sits under it. Its cells are half the page header's height and one line runs along the header's rule. It's decoration only: behind the content, hidden from screen readers, never on cards. data-grid="off" on the root turns it off (D81).

Bars that make way

Scrolling down, the chrome slides out of the way; it comes back as soon as you scroll up, a menu or sheet opens, or focus goes to a field up top. What sticks under it (a toolbar, a table's column header, the record beside a list) moves with it, so nothing is left hanging. The shell does this once for every page (BolsterShell.scrolling, D73); the page is the one thing that scrolls (Page scrolling).

The first design, in the system

Measured on Bolster's live app (Oct 2026) and kept close to it, with a narrower section sidebar (D24, D26).

Rail

60 wide, on the chrome, with 44px items: a 16px Light icon in a 20px box. Pointed at, it widens to 232 over the page with a shadow and shows each item's name; it opens after 150ms, so crossing it on the way to the sidebar doesn't flash it, and closes 100ms after the pointer leaves. Keyboard focus opens it at once and Escape closes it. Pin sidebar at its foot keeps it 232 wide in the layout, from 1024 up (D25).

Section sidebar

The current section's pages, on the light cement beside the rail: a 64px header with the section's name in the display face, 36px items with 14px medium labels, small group labels, and the current page white with a soft shadow. It's 256 wide in every section, the longest label (“Leads and customers”) plus 48, so the page doesn't shift between sections (D26).

Bottom tab bar and action bar

Below 768 a tab bar (.bottom-bar) takes the rail's place at the foot, 60 tall: icons over 10px labels, the current one in full ink with a yellow icon. A page's sticky footer of actions (.action-bar, a proposal's Decline and Approve) sits above it, never under it; Bolster's tab bar covered the proposal's Approve button.

Trying out: one dark menu

In the concepts, one sidebar replaces the rail and the section sidebar, after Shopify's admin: fewer levels of navigation for most of Bolster (D88). It's what every concept page uses today, and it's still being judged.

  • Every section in one list. Getting started, Dashboard, then Jobs, Financial and Customers as accordions (one open at a time, the current page's open on arrival), then Marketing. A section with pages has a chevron at its row's end that turns down as it opens. The pill marks the page you're on, or its section's row while that section is folded.
  • A foot that stays put: Messages, Files, Help and Settings, with Collapse menu under them, while the rest scrolls (D118, D107).
  • Dark, in both themes, with three steps of ink: the section you're in brightest, other sections a step down, a section's pages quieter still. Section rows are 44 tall, as the rail's are; pages are 32, each with a 14px icon a step below its section's 16 (D93, D95). The menu draws Regular icons, a weight up from the rest of the chrome's Light.
  • Folded by default to the icon rail; opening it is remembered in the browser (D115). Folded, pointing at a section opens its pages in a popup beside the rail, after 60ms; a row without pages names itself in the same popup. Expand menu at the rail's foot opens the menu again (D107).
  • The logo grows: the yellow mark becomes the wordmark as the menu opens, its cut-out turning into the B, the letters rising one after another over 420ms (D91).
  • Settings is a window, full screen on phones: its groups and pages on the left, the page itself on the right, with a button in its header that hides and shows that navigation (D88, D89).
  • Below 1024, no bars. No top bar and no tab bar: one floating menu button at the bottom left, across from Create, opens the same menu, pushing the page aside to an 88px column. The button rides into that column as ✕; a tap in the column, a link or Escape closes it. Search, Ask Bolton and your account move into the menu; its rows are 44 tall with 16px type. On a tablet the menu is 272 wide. While rows are ticked the button steps aside for the bulk bar (I9).

Try it in any concept, such as the job list: point at the rail, open a section, then narrow the window below 1024.

Usage

  • A page never draws chrome or copies the shell's behaviour; it mounts the shell and passes what's its own. Behaviour two pages share moves into the shell first.
  • The page's actions go in its page header, the main one last. The top bar holds only what belongs to every page: the company, search, Ask Bolton and your account.
  • Icons in the chrome are Font Awesome Light; icons in the page are Regular. Search and help are Regular in both (D84). The dark menu being tried draws Regular (D93).
  • Section and page names are Bolster's own, as the live app labels them.

Accessibility

  • Each navigation is a nav with its own label (“Main”, the section's name); the current page is marked aria-current="page".
  • The rail's names are real text in its links, shown as it widens, so they need no tooltips to be read (D25). Keyboard focus opens the rail at once; Escape closes it.
  • In the menu being tried, a folded section's row opens its popup on focus; Tab or ArrowRight goes in, arrows move, Escape or ArrowLeft come back to the row (I9).
  • The floating menu button is named “Menu”, and “Close menu” while open, with aria-expanded; closing with Escape puts focus back on it.
  • The canvas grid is aria-hidden and takes no pointer events. Reduced motion stops the accordions, the logo and the menu sliding; they switch instead.
