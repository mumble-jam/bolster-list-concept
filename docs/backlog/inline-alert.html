Inline alert

A message that stays on the page until it's dealt with: information, a warning, a success, an error.

Not built yetHigh priority

Not built yet. Bolster needs it and the system doesn't have it. This page is the entry for it: what it's for, where Bolster needs it, and what has to be settled before it's built.

What it's for

Toasts leave; some things mustn't. A trial ending, a payment that failed, an integration that stopped syncing, a form that couldn't save: each needs a message on the page, in the place it's about.

Where Bolster needs it

  • Settings and billing (~/billing, ~/payment-settings)
  • Forms: a summary of what couldn't save
  • Integrations (~/integrations): sync errors
  • A record that's locked or read-only

What it has to do

  • Four tones on the feedback tokens, each with its icon (never colour alone)
  • A title, a line, at most two actions; dismissible only when nothing is lost
  • Errors announced (role="alert"), the rest polite
  • Inside a card, its corners nest (D35), as the error banner's do

What exists today

  • The error banner (states.css), danger only
  • The feedback tokens: surface, content and accent for each tone

Open questions

  • Page-wide notices (a trial ending): an inline alert at the top of the page, or a bar in the shell?
