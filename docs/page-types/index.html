Page types

Whole pages put together from the system's components and patterns: what each page type is for, how it behaves, and which of Bolster's pages it covers.

Bolster has about 250 pages, but far fewer kinds of page. The audit folded them into thirteen page types. A page type here is one of them built for real from the system, with its parts, its behaviour and the questions it still has.

One is stable: the list page, which covers more of Bolster than any other and is built by a recipe from a model and an intent. Four more are being tried out as concepts, each on the list's parts: the to-do list, the calendar, the pipeline and the timeline. The estimate is a first draft. The rest aren't built yet; each has a page saying what it's for, where Bolster needs it and what has to be settled.

Outside the thirteen, the audit's templates also list the public pages clients and vendors open (client and vendor portal), sign in (Sign in) and the imports and flows (Wizard and import).

Every page type

  • List pageStableJobs, invoices, vendors, the catalog: cards, a table card, the record beside it; built from a model and an intent.
  • To-doTrying outTasks as a list of things to do: grouped by when, done in one click, set in the row.
  • CalendarTrying outMonth, week, day and crew: jobs' phases as bars, appointments by time, unscheduled work to drag on.
  • PipelineTrying outOne board from the first enquiry to the paid invoice: people and jobs said plainly, money per stage.
  • TimelineTrying outA gantt of jobs and their stages: brackets, bars, milestones, links, scrolled with the page.
  • EstimateDraftAn estimate's list view: assemblies, items, labour and selections as a tree, an inspector, the job's total.
  • Home and getting startedNot built yetWhere a company lands: what needs them today, and on day one, the set-up checklist.
  • DashboardNot built yetHow the business is doing: sales, jobs and money, with page-wide filters.
  • Job workspaceNot built yetEverything about one job in one window: its estimate, schedule, money, files and people.
  • SettingsNot built yetThe company's set-up: sections, forms and how they save.
  • Document and presentationNot built yetWhat the client sees: the proposal, the estimate and the invoice, on screen and as a PDF.
  • MessagingNot built yetConversations with clients and vendors, tied to their jobs.
  • FilesNot built yetEvery photo and document, by job: browse, preview, share.
  • ReportsNot built yetTables and charts to answer one question at a time, exported.
  • Wizard and importNot built yetA task in steps: importing clients or a catalog, setting up a flow.
  • Field appNot built yetBolster on a phone on a job site: today's jobs, logs, tasks, photos, chat.
  • Client and vendor portalNot built yetThe pages people outside the company see: a proposal to approve, an invoice to pay, a bid request to answer.
  • Sign inNot built yetSign in, reset a password, accept an invitation.
