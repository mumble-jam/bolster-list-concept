Page types

Estimate

A job's estimate in its List view: assemblies, items, labour and selections as a tree, an inspector for the chosen line, how far the estimate is and the job's total.

Draftconcepts/estimate/index.htmlconcepts/estimate/estimate.cssconcepts/estimate/estimate.jsUpdated 2026-10-09

In Bolster an estimate is built inside the job's workspace: the full-screen window a job opens into, with its own navigation (Overview, Estimate, Takeoff, Proposal presentation, Selections, and the production pages). The estimate concept redraws one view of it, the List view, in the list page's table styling: the same card, rules, hovers and measured columns, with a tree on top.

A first draft. It was built early (Round 1, 9 Oct), before the system settled its status quo and the page-scrolling rule, and it loads the list concept's own CSS rather than the system's list page. Read it as a direction, not as the system. There are no approved screenshots of it yet.

Try it: the estimate concept, a made-up estimate for “Roof and kitchen refresh”, a booked job.

Who it's for

The estimator pricing a job: setting quantities, making the selections that are theirs to make, and getting the estimate ready to send. The customer makes the other selections when they open the proposal.

The tree

The tree's shape is Bolster's: assemblies hold items, labour and selections, nested to any depth, each level stepping in 28.

  • An assembly is a section of the estimate: a dark tile, its name, its category after it (“Roofing – Shingle”), a chevron to fold it. Its cost and price add up what's in it.
  • An item has a thumbnail (a photo in Bolster; here a tile with its trade's icon), its name, a description line, and an AutoCost tag where its price is kept up to date from the supplier.
  • Labour is a line of its own, with the hammer.
  • A selection is a choice: what it chooses follows its name, and a lock says the estimator decides it rather than the customer. Without a quantity it shows Set qty.

When a line is short of room, what it chooses or its category gives way first; the name truncates only when it's longer than the whole line.

Behaviour

WhatHow
Opening a lineA click on a row, or Enter on its name, opens it in the inspector: beside the estimate from 1280 wide (the Cost column steps aside), a sheet below. Focus goes to its title; Close or Escape returns it to the name.
FoldingThe chevron before anything with children folds and unfolds it, focus staying on it.
SelectingTicking an assembly ticks everything in it; an assembly with only some of it ticked shows a dash. The header's checkbox selects the whole estimate.
ColumnsEach number column is as wide as its widest value across the whole estimate (I13); Project scope takes the rest.
PhonesRows become cards, keeping their indent and fold; the AutoCost tag and the lock wait for the inspector. The foot sticks to the bottom.

Not in the concept: editing a quantity or a price, Sheet and Cards, Dimensions, search, and Preview and send.
